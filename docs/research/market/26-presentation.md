### Can the page name Anki? I am not a lawyer

**Apple's rules** ([guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), read 2026-11-05):
- **2.3.7:** a unique name, keywords that describe the app, no metadata packed with trademarked terms or popular app names to game the system. Subtitles must not "reference other apps".
- **4.1(c):** no other developer's icon, brand or product name in your icon or name without approval.
- **5.2.1:** no protected third-party material such as trademarks without permission; nothing misleading or copycat in names or metadata.
- **2.3 and 2.3.1:** metadata must be accurate.
- **Keywords:** [product page guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/) bars unauthorized trademarks and competing app names; [App Store Connect help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information) bars names of other apps or companies. [Search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/) indexes title, subtitle, keywords and category.

**Reading** `[INFERRED]`: name, subtitle and keywords are closed by text. No rule addresses a factual compatibility sentence in the description. 5.2.1's "without permission" is broad enough to be used against one. I found no case either way.

**Practice:** the subtitles above appear to break 2.3.7 and stay live, so being live proves little. A [2018 developer](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/111168) `[OLD]` says he was rejected under 2.3.7 while rivals passed, 1 report. [Gummicube](https://www.gummicube.com/blog/targeting-competitor-ios-app-brands-with-keyword-optimization) (Aug 2024, `[OLD]`, sells ASO) says brands can't go in descriptions, stricter than the text, and that brand keywords often pass, with risk. Rights holders can complain through Apple's form (5.2).

**The mark:**
- `[CLAIMED]` [Anki's site](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) and [AnkiMobile docs](https://docs.ankiweb.net/ankimobile/intro): a registered trademark of Ankitects Pty Ltd.
- Developer of Anki, [Sep 2025](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/question-about-api-access-naming-anki-cards-in-external-app/66116) `[SAID]`: registered in several countries after years; copycats had to rename.
- US: serial 79340880, registration 7838778, June 24, 2025, classes 9 and 42, from an aggregator's search snippet; unverified at USPTO.
- A [separate ANKI filing](https://trea.com/information/98939510---anki/trademark/2da5829d-68bc-4ee5-9024-230b2dfded84) by Digital Dream Labs covers robots and toys, suspended June 2025.
- Possible change: [business operations are moving to AnkiHub](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankis-growing-up/68610.md) (Feb 2026), and AnkiMobile's seller is now Anki Software LLC. I found nothing on who will hold the mark.

**Anki's position:** I found no written name policy. Signals, 3 threads and 1 FAQ:
- In the Sep 2025 thread above, a paid platform asked about its "Anki Cards" section and a disclaimer: trademark of Ankitects, not affiliated or endorsed. Anki's developer approved the disclaimer. That is the only maintainer statement on such wording. The feature was free and the asker said he wasn't competing.
- A community member, not official, same thread and [Jul 2026](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/own-re-created-anki-app/70522): any app that reviews competes with Anki; standalone apps should drop the name from theirs.
- [Apr 2026](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/requesting-to-add-a-new-anki-add-on-to-ankiweb/69372): a forum regular with an official tone told an add-on maker to rethink a name suggesting a link; the same community member: volunteer projects are tolerated, for-profit or competing ones risk trademark trouble.
- [Knockoffs FAQ](https://docs.ankiweb.net/faqs/anki-knockoffs.md): AlgoApp and Noji aren't part of the ecosystem and their names may trade on Anki's; it asks people to warn others in reviews.

**"Compatible with":**
- Only Renki and NotchWise use compatibility wording; AnkiDroid says it for itself.
- US nominative fair use (law-firm summaries, through search; [one attorney's piece](https://www.patenttrademarkblog.com/?p=693), 2023 `[OLD]`): needed to identify the product, only as much as needed, no suggestion of sponsorship. Compatibility is a listed example; "recommended" is risky.
- EU: Article 14(1)(c) EUTMR lets a mark indicate intended purpose, within honest practices. [CMS on Audi v GQ](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/pre-branded-spare-parts-cjeu-has-its-say-in-audi-trade-mark-case) (Mar 2024) `[OLD]`: telling what a product is for stays allowed; mimicking the brand doesn't. Not about apps.
- A model from practice: [Apple's own trademark rules](https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html): word mark, no logo, not in the product's name, a phrase like "compatible with", less prominent than your name, only if true.

**Short answer** `[INFERRED]`: not in name, subtitle or keywords. A plain factual line in the description has common practice and doctrine behind it, but no explicit permission, and Apple can still object. The closest thing to Anki's approval is one forum reply. Written permission from Anki's current owners would settle it.

## Evidence against

- **Against "Anki enforces":** Renki and AnkiCard carry the name in 2026, after registration. AlgoApp's page gives no reason for renaming. "Under trademark pressure" comes from [Mindomax](https://www.mindomax.com/anki-pro-vs-anki), which sells a rival; per a store snippet, Noji called it a refresh. I found no suit or takedown.
- **Against "registered":** one forum poster doubted it in Aug 2025; the developer corrected it. No USPTO page read.
- **Against "AI is the only pitch":** Anki, Mochi, Memrise, Readwise and Vocabulary don't lead with it.
- **Against the empty space:** empty may mean no demand. Anki's developer doubted widgets (report 20), and the small apps there show few ratings.
- **Against pages mattering:** AnkiMobile sells with the weakest page. Claims that the first shots carry 70–80% of conversion come from vendor blogs with no study; I don't count them.
- **Against honesty as a pattern:** RemNote's [help comparison](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6025618-remnote-vs-anki-supermemo-and-other-spaced-repetition-tools) admits no shortcoming. Vocabulary's clean page sits beside tracking labels.
- **Against this sample:** shots come from the API's default set; prices vary by storefront.
