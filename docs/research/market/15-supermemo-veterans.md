# 15 supermemo-veterans
Sources read: about 50 pages (through WebFetch's summarizer), plus search snippets, marked as such. Covered from 1993 to 2026.

## Findings

### Live: SuperMemo for Windows (1987; company 1991)
- **Who:** people who want incremental reading and a maximal system; `[CLAIMED]` [spaced repetition, incremental reading, concept maps, sleep analysis](https://help.supermemo.org/wiki/Features) (edited 2026).
- **Advantages:** `[CLAIMED]` [Algorithm SM-20, Algorithm Arena, PDF editor](https://help.supermemo.org/wiki/Four_Decades:_From_SuperMemo_1.0_to_SuperMemo_19.0) in version 20.
- **Complaints:** hard to learn or ugly, in 8 of 9 voices, a thin sample. `[SAID]` [2 AlternativeTo reviews, 2021](https://alternativeto.net/software/supermemo-desktop/about/); [a Hacker News comment, 2024](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41913751); [a LessWrong user](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/YbCc3NRrr5avvWSHT/who-wants-to-start-an-important-startup) `[OLD, ~2012]`; a [one-month test](https://momomori-eki.net/2021/01/08/battle-of-the-spaced-repetition-heavyweights-anki-vs-supermemo-part-ii/) (2021) that quit after a corrupted backup; second-hand snippets of a [2018 Reddit post](https://nyc1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/Anki/comments/9bvngf/why_i_switched_to_supermemo_after_using_anki_for) and others. The ninth, a [WaniKani poster](https://community.wanikani.com/t/anki-vs-supermemo/50766) (2021), moved to it for the lighter load but calls it far from perfect. A co-founder said the same in [2001](https://www-v1.supermemo.com/archives1990-2015/articles/interview) `[OLD]`: a weak Windows interface and no usability expert ever hired.
- **Price:** `[CLAIMED]` [$64 for SuperMemo 20, $28 for 19, $88 bundled with Advanced English](https://supermemo.store/) (read 2026-11-05). One-time licence. `[CLAIMED]` [older versions become freeware](https://super-memory.com/articles/soft/free.htm), and 5-10% of registered users started there (undated, likely `[OLD]`).
- **Why people move:** to it for incremental reading (1 WaniKani poster); away to Anki. SuperMemo's own wiki says Anki [beat SuperMemo in popularity](https://supermemo.guru/wiki/Anki) soon after emerging `[CLAIMED, edited 2020, OLD]`. Anki's manual calls SuperMemo [proprietary, with no access to raw data](https://docs.ankiweb.net/background.html) `[CLAIMED, a rival's words]`.
- **Notifications, widgets, lock screen, menu bar, watch:** none on its [features page](https://help.supermemo.org/wiki/Features). Windows only; Mac only through [third-party CrossOver reports](https://codeweavers.com/compatibility/crossover/supermemo-17), `[OLD]`. Phone review of incremental reading is [planned](https://www.supermemo.guru/wiki/36th_anniversary_of_SuperMemo) (2025).
- **First sentence:** store: "Discover SuperMemo: 42 years of memory research in a powerful learning system."
- **Alive:** yes. [SuperMemo 20, 2026-04-01](https://supermemo.guru/wiki/SuperMemo_20); [20.00.32, 2026-06-29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperMemo).

### Live: SuperMemo.com app (same company, a different product)
- **Who:** language learners. `[CLAIMED]` [25 languages, AI tutor, MemoChat](https://www.supermemo.com/en); the store says [27 others plus English](https://apps.apple.com/pl/app/supermemo-com/id982498980).
- **Complaints:** of 4 reviews visible on the [Polish store page](https://apps.apple.com/pl/app/supermemo-com/id982498980), 1 says dated (2022), 1 a surprise 1,000-card limit after an update (2023; the developer replied that premium raises it), 1 too few exercises, 1 word order. A [US-store snippet](https://apps.apple.com/py/app/supermemo-effective-learning/id982498980?l=en-GB) objects to about $10 a month and old design. [An affiliate review, 2024](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/supermemo/), scores it 3.0, "okay". [Trustpilot](https://ch.trustpilot.com/review/supermemo.com) has 1 review, so no signal.
- **Price:** `[CLAIMED]` first month free; [35.99 zł a month, 359.99 zł a year, courses 79.90-249.90 zł](https://apps.apple.com/pl/app/supermemo-com/id982498980); US snippet: $9.99 and $99.99. Five discount codes appear in search-result titles from its site (PREMIUMX20, 35YEARS, BACKTOSCHOOL26, EXTREMESALE, FASTTRACK30): `[INFERRED]` discounting is routine.
- **Surfaces:** no watch or widget listed; "improved notification appearance" in a [2025 release note](https://apps.apple.com/pl/app/supermemo-com/id982498980).
- **First sentence:** site: ["Which language would you like to learn?"](https://www.supermemo.com/en), under the headline "Learn fast and remember forever!". Store: "SuperMemo – Language Learning with AI Learn English and 27 other languages with SuperMemo!"
- **Alive:** yes. 4.6 from 2.9k ratings in Poland; version 2.1.22, 28 Oct, year not shown (`[INFERRED]` 2025 or 2026); [a site article dated 2026-05-26](https://www.supermemo.com/en?p=161597).

### Live: Mnemosyne (2006; free, AGPL, volunteer)
- **Who:** people who want a free desktop tool, and memory researchers; `[CLAIMED]` it [collects volunteers' data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mnemosyne_(software)).
- **Complaints:** 5 of about 14 recent [mailing-list titles](https://groups.google.com/g/mnemosyne-proj-users) (Nov 2025 to Nov 2026) are about sync or Android failures, plus install or launch problems on Windows and Mac. No iPhone app was found; the [Android app is a review client synced from the desktop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mnemosyne_(software)).
- **Price:** free. No notifications, widgets, menu-bar item or watch app appear in anything I read.
- **Why people stay:** `[SAID]` 2 users on the list: one calls it ["the backbone of my continued learning"](https://groups.google.com/g/mnemosyne-proj-users/c/TJBYABcp4t4) (2026); one snippet says over 10 years of daily use.
- **First sentence:** README: "a free, open-source, spaced-repetition flashcard program that helps you learn as efficiently as possible." [Website](https://mnemosyne-proj.org/) on 2026-11-05: "The hosting account for mnemosyne-proj.org has been suspended." (read twice, undated; I found no explanation).
- **Alive:** barely, and changing hands. [Last release 2.11, 2023-11-12](https://github.com/mnemosyne-proj/mnemosyne/releases). [Commits until 2026-04-18](https://github.com/mnemosyne-proj/mnemosyne/commits/master): HTTPS sync, Android and macOS fixes. A list snippet has the long-time maintainer saying he stepped down and a new one took over (`[INFERRED]` 2026; I could not read the thread itself). A 6-message thread of 4 Nov on the project's [long-term viability](https://groups.google.com/g/mnemosyne-proj-users/search?q=Future%20of%20Mnemosyne) mentions a GitHub fork with Apple Silicon build steps. [605 stars](https://github.com/mnemosyne-proj/mnemosyne).

### Live, but left consumers: Cerego (born as iKnow)
`[CLAIMED]` ["Cerego uses AI to make learning orders of magnitude more effective."](https://www.cerego.com/) It sells to enterprise, education and government through "Schedule a Demo", with no public price, and claims 500+ organizations. Its [origin was a Japanese English-learning service](https://kwx.liehendr.com/content/wikipedia_en_all_maxi_2024-01/A/Cerego) (snippet of a Wikipedia mirror). Its clients skew institutional, so I sought no consumer complaints.

### Closed
**Tinycards (Duolingo, 2016 to 2020-09-01)**
- **Why:** `[CLAIMED]` Duolingo wanted to [focus resources on its main app](https://thelanguagecloset.com/2020/10/17/%f0%9f%91%8f%f0%9f%8f%bb-method-%f0%9f%91%8f%f0%9f%8f%bb-review-the-short-lived-tinycards-2016-2020/) (a blogger's paraphrase, 2020; Duolingo's own post was not reachable). `[INFERRED]` It was free and I found no revenue of its own.
- **How:** [announced June 2020](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/tinycards-to-kartka-migration-tool), about 3 months' notice, deck creation switched off first.
- **What users did:** [503 people signed a petition](https://www.change.org/p/duolingo-duolingo-tinycards-should-not-be-taken-down) asking Duolingo to sell it, charge for it or take donations. They had reason to worry: [no export in 2017](https://felixwong.com/2017/08/how-to-export-tinycards-by-duolingo-to-quizlet-csv-excel/). A startup, Kartka, built a [migration tool](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/tinycards-to-kartka-migration-tool) to win them; `kartka.ai` no longer resolves (2026-11-05). `[SAID, second-hand]` [Reddit snippets, 2020](https://red.127.pp.ua/r/languagelearning/comments/hctrb9/tinycards_shutting_down_september_1st_2020): 1 moved to Memrise with a CSV; 1 found Anki too complicated and wanted a friendlier app. `[CLAIMED]` [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/tinycards/about) lists Anki, Duolingo and RemNote. I have no numbers on where most went.
- **Liked for:** `[SAID]` [looks and ease](https://mickmel.substack.com/p/tinycards-vs-anki) (1 Anki fan, 2017 `[OLD]`), though capped at 150 cards a deck.

**StudyBlue (2006/2009 to end of 2020)**
- **Money:** [a $3.6M round in 2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StudyBlue); [Pro at $9.99](https://www.educationalappstore.com/app/studyblue) per a stale directory; [sold to Chegg in 2018 for $20.8M in cash](https://vistapointadvisors.com/news/chegg-acquires-studyblue), the seller's advisor's figure. Chegg [promised no interruption](https://ycharts.com/news/story/PRN-NY43824-20180702) (snippet) and valued its [20M+ flashcards](https://www.dvsd.org/Page/688) (snippet of a school page).
- **Why it closed:** [discontinued about two years later](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StudyBlue); no reason given anywhere I could read. `[INFERRED]` Its library and data were what was bought, not the app.
- **What users did:** [Chegg Prep's reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1462189360/chegg-prep-study-flashcards/reviews), collected by an aggregator: about 14 of 33 listed report lost decks or cards; several say the old apps (Flashcards+, StudyBlue) were cut off and they were forced across without warning; one lost 5.5 years of vocabulary (2023). A directory [points to Quizlet, Brainscape and AnkiApp](https://www.educationalappstore.com/app/studyblue).
- **The buyer since:** Chegg [cut 45% of staff in Oct 2025](https://www.aol.com/articles/chegg-cuts-45-staff-ai-051224772.html), blaming AI and lost Google traffic; its [Q2 2026 revenue fell 51%](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001364954/000136495426000088/chgg-20260630.htm).

**Smaller deaths**
- [Kindle Flashcards](https://goodereader.com/blog/?p=384115): removed August to November 2024, no reason given, and no export mentioned.
- Polar Bookshelf: 1 Product Hunt reviewer (~2024; link left out, it carries a username) says the open-source version vanished and updates stopped; unconfirmed.
- [zuknow](https://alternativeto.net/software/studyblue/?p=2): marked Discontinued, nothing more known.
- Memrise's community courses [moved off the app in 2024](https://www.memrise.com/blog/changes-to-the-memrise-app) (angle 16 covers this).

### What these histories teach `[INFERRED, from the above]`
1. **Free flashcards inside a bigger company die when it refocuses.** Tinycards, StudyBlue, Flashcards+ and Kindle (4 of 4 such cases I found) had owners with another main business, and the closures came 2 to 4 years after launch or purchase, where dates exist.
2. **Small paid tools outlive funded ones.** Mental Case/Studies has run 20 years on paid upgrades: its developer said [without upgrade income "we would have shut down"](https://mentalfaculty.tenderapp.com/discussions/suggestions/10061-mental-case-replaced-by-studies) (2016 `[OLD]`; 1 of 3 customers in the thread objected to paying again). Flashcards Deluxe runs on purchase and donations. Survivorship bias applies: dead indie apps leave little trace.
3. **The tool alone earned little, even for the inventor.** SuperMemo's partner [said profitability "has been a disaster most of the time"](https://www-v1.supermemo.com/archives1990-2015/articles/interview) (2001 `[OLD]`); total sales only [passed $2M by 1999](https://www-v1.supermemo.com/archives1990-2015/english/company/smworld) (snippet); [about 50,000 sales at €30 in 2006](https://jastaursula.substack.com/p/supermemo) (a 2025 blog whose author offers study help). Its 1996 [US company aimed at NASDAQ and closed](https://supermemo.guru/wiki/History_of_SuperMemo). Earlier, [nearly failed](https://supermemo.guru/wiki/How_SuperMemo_World_nearly_failed) in 1991 for lack of credibility and channels, saved by press after a contest. Shareware brought [a handful of payments](https://supermemo.guru/wiki/History_of_SuperMemo) (snippet). What sold was content and bundles: [SuperMemo 98 "sold over a million copies"](https://help.supermemo.org/wiki/Four_Decades:_From_SuperMemo_1.0_to_SuperMemo_19.0) as a multimedia partwork, OEM deals, a [T-Mobile campaign in 2012](https://supermemo.guru/wiki/History_of_SuperMemo). Today's consumer face sells language courses; Cerego moved to institutions; Chegg is moving to B2B skilling.
4. **The habit is the wall.** Founders in [2001](https://www-v1.supermemo.com/archives1990-2015/articles/interview): not viral, few people enjoy learning, daily practice feels like a burden, and piracy never was the main headache they expected. [A LessWrong user](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/YbCc3NRrr5avvWSHT/who-wants-to-start-an-important-startup) describes most quitting at a two-week hump (1 voice, `[OLD]`).
5. **The algorithm didn't protect the leader.** SM-2 was public and copied; a free, multi-platform app with a far older algorithm passed the inventor (see Anki above).
6. **Platform bets cost decades.** SuperMemo relied on Internet Explorer for about [two decades until SuperMemo 19 in 2023](https://supermemo.guru/wiki/SuperMemo_19), and stayed on Windows.
7. **Volunteer tools hang on one person.** Mnemosyne's handover and suspended site show it, though its users stayed about 20 years.
8. **Closure's sting is the data.** Lost cards are what reviews complain of, more than the lost app; the Tinycards petition even proposed charging for it.

## Evidence against
- **SuperMemo isn't dying.** Its 40-year run, a 2026 major version and [Beginner and Basic levels](https://help.supermemo.org/wiki/Features) go against "too hard to survive". The Internet Explorer complaint, repeated on Hacker News in 2024, was stale after SuperMemo 19 (2023). Its complaint sample is 9 voices, mostly old.
- **The loud "lost data" count is a selection.** Chegg Prep's 33 listed reviews are mostly negative, while the same page shows 4.6 and 63.6% positive across 3,102 reviews, and some say bugs are fixed. Whether StudyBlue's own users, as opposed to Flashcards+'s, lost data is unconfirmed: I couldn't read the sunset page.
- **Tinycards' mourners were few:** 503 signatures, 3 AlternativeTo comments. A 2020 reviewer gave it [6.5/10](https://thelanguagecloset.com/2020/10/17/%f0%9f%91%8f%f0%9f%8f%bb-method-%f0%9f%91%8f%f0%9f%8f%bb-review-the-short-lived-tinycards-2016-2020/). Duolingo may have let people download decks (a Reddit snippet), against the 2017 "no export".
- **"Not viral" is 2001 talk.** Anki and Duolingo have since spread spaced repetition widely.
- **Algorithm vs. ease:** the one-person test favouring Anki (91.8% vs 65.7% accuracy) covered one month, and SuperMemo's reviews took less time. [A Hacker News comment](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41913751) says incremental reading never reached Anki, so SuperMemo keeps something unique.
- **Chegg's fall is not a flashcard story:** it's homework help hit by AI. And being big isn't fatal in itself: Quizlet (angle 13) is alive.
- **Lesson 2 rests on a few small apps** with few ratings and no revenue data.
