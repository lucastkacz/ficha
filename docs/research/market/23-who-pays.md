# 23 who-pays
Sources read: about 55 (some short; about 15 more seen only as search snippets, and marked so). Covered from 2013 to 2026.

How solid: **A** audited or filed. **B** a company's own statement or a developer's own numbers, unaudited. **C** a tracker's modeled estimate, often off by 4x or more. **D** unsourced or from a report mill. `[OLD]` means before 2024-11-05.

## Findings

### Who pays, and for what?

I found no survey of who pays for flashcard apps. All of this is pieced together.

- **Language learners are the largest proven payers.** Duolingo had 13.1M paid subscribers of 153.1M monthly users in Q3 2026, and subscriptions were about 87% of its $309.0M revenue ([SEC filing, Nov 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)). A. `[INFERRED]` That is about 8.6% of monthly users, and about $82 per subscriber per year. In US education apps, Language, Kids Education and Studying Tools led spending, within 7 share points ([Sensor Tower, 2022](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-education-apps-us-2022) `[OLD]`). C.
- **School and college students are the largest audience, and pay resentfully.** Quizlet says 2 in 3 US high school and 1 in 2 college students use it, 60M+ people in all ([press summary, July 2025](https://techintelpro.com/news/ai/enterprise-ai/quizlets-how-america-learns-report-highlights-ai-and-digital-learning-trends)). B, `[CLAIMED]`. No paying share is published. The 5.2M paid subscribers on a template site has no source, so I discard it ([snippet](https://canvasbusinessmodel.com/products/quizlet-swot-analysis)). D. Plus costs $35.99 a year; students on a teacher's set get every feature free, which Quizlet's then CEO said encourages teacher use ([student paper, 2022](https://machronicle.com/?p=72892) `[OLD]`). Trustpilot: 1.4/5 from 500+ reviews, as reported by a site that [sells tutoring](https://www.myengineeringbuddy.com/blog/quizlet-reviews-alternatives-pricing-offerings/) (Oct 2026). `[SAID]`, and complainers self-select.
- **Medical students pay most reliably, and more than once.** 68.3% of 560 US students reported using Anki ([national survey, via snippet](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8651966), 2020 `[OLD]`), and they spent 63% of their time on third-party resources in another study ([snippet](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11002966/table/T3) `[OLD]`). No dollar survey found; low response rates and self-selection apply.
- **Professionals pay for exam question banks, not general flashcards.** Pocket Prep sells 1, 3 and 12-month Premium ([FAQ](https://www.pocketprep.com/frequently-asked-questions/), snippet); AMBOSS sells yearly or monthly ([pricing](https://www.amboss.com/int/pricing), snippet). `[CLAIMED]`. No revenue or subscriber numbers. `[INFERRED]` The money follows an exam date.
- **Hobbyists pay little and rarely, as far as I can see.** In one HN search, about 4 of 8 Anki-related comments would pay and about 4 refuse because Anki is free; the rest were about other tools, and most are `[OLD]` ([search](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=anki%20would%20pay%20for&tags=comment&hitsPerPage=40), first 100,000 of 153,000 characters). `[SAID]` Tech-heavy, small.
- **Parents: no evidence for flashcards.** Kids Education money is ABCmouse-style subscriptions ([Sensor Tower, 2022](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-education-apps-us-2022) `[OLD]`). Quizlet's report counts parents as respondents, not payers. `[INFERRED]`
- **Report-mill split, for scale only:** K-12 42%, higher education 35%, language learners and professionals 23% ([PW Consulting](https://pmarketresearch.com/it/flashcard-app-market)). D, no source shown.

### One-time or subscription

**Across all apps, subscriptions take the money, but one-time is growing.**
- One-time, including lifetime, rose from 6.4% of revenue in 2023 to 10.3% in 2025, while weekly plans hold 55.6% ([Adapty, 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-report/), sells paywalls). 23.2% of apps sell subscriptions plus lifetime ([RevenueCat, 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/), sells subscription tools, its own customers only). `[CLAIMED]`
- **Education specifically:** median $44.99 a year, the highest of any category, and $9.99 a month; 6.5% download-to-trial; $0.30 revenue per install by day 14; among the hardest categories to break through ([RevenueCat Education](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-education)). One-year subscriber value $45.10, install value $1.06 ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-report/)).
- **Most apps earn almost nothing.** About $72 a month one year after launch across categories; 17.3% reach $1K a month within two years, 4.6% reach $10K ([RevenueCat, 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)). Same picture in 2024: under $50 ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2024/03/13/most-subscription-apps-do-not-make-money/) `[OLD]`).

### How big could a small paid app honestly get?

`[INFERRED]` from the above, loosely:
- **Most:** under $1K a month. About 83% of new subscription apps never reach it within two years.
- **A solid solo result:** $50K-150K a year: Lunar's studio, Launch Center Pro's peak, VirtualHostX in its best year. Each took years, and two lean on selling outside the App Store.
- **Rare:** about $1M a year: Slopes after nine years; Launch Center Pro only in lifetime gross.
- **Small teams:** Ulysses at 50K subscribers. At $39.99 list and with many at half price, that's perhaps $1-2M a year. Brainscape about $6M est. with 40 staff. Vocabulary, 16.2M iOS installs, $50K+ a month, half from ads ([AppGoblin](https://www.appgoblin.info/apps/1084540807)), or $200K ([ScreensDesign snippet](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/vocabulary-learn-words-daily)). C.
- **What companies sold for:** StudyBlue, flashcards, $20.8M cash in 2018 ([Chegg 10-Q snippet](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001364954/000136495418000170/chegg2018-06x3010q.htm) `[OLD]`). A. Drops, 25M users, 21 staff, $31M plus up to $19M in 2020 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/11/24/kahoot-drops-50m-on-drops-to-add-language-learning-to-its-gamified-education-stable/) `[OLD]`).
- **Paying math:** $50K a year at $10 a month needs 417 subscribers ([forum](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/why-do-developers-prefer-subscriptions/40177)). `[INFERRED]` At $25 once, after a 15% cut, about 2,350 new buyers every year, for ever. Demand for a paid app tapers once interested people have bought, one developer argues ([HN, Aug 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49266416)). `[SAID]` One voice.
- **The niche's ceiling:** Readwise bootstrapped on the view that its niche had "hundreds of thousands" of US users ([blog, 2018](https://blog.readwise.io/why-were-bootstrapping-readwise/) `[OLD]`). No current numbers.
- Mochi, often named as the paid indie alternative, has no public revenue; a tracker shows 29K iOS installs ([AppGoblin](https://www.appgoblin.info/apps/1507775056)). C. I found no founder-disclosed revenue for any flashcard app.

## Evidence against
- **"One-time works":** one-time is still only 10.3% of revenue, and it includes consumables. Every one-time success above but AnkiMobile is small, old, or sold outside the App Store. An unverified claim says subscription apps earn 4.5x more per user ([Swift Kit](https://theswiftk.it.com/blog/how-to-monetize-ios-app-indie-developer), sells a starter kit).
- **"Subscriptions win":** those figures come from subscription vendors, and exclude paid apps that never used them. AnkiMobile sits in the top 5 with no subscription.
- **"Anki being free means nobody pays":** AnkiMobile's rank and AnkiHub's staff both say otherwise. But the charts rank downloads, and I have no dollar figure.
- **"Students will keep paying":** Chegg lost 31% of subscribers in a year, blaming AI tools. Yet Quizlet's estimates rise after its paywall, so anger hasn't visibly cost it, if Latka is right.
- **"AI makers are the money":** AI apps earn 41% more per payer but churn 30% faster ([RevenueCat, 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)). Gizmo's revenue is reported as both under $1M and $25M.
- **Tracker estimates:** Brainscape's iOS figure differs 6x or more between sites, and Latka's labels are unclear. Treat all class C as an order of magnitude at best.
