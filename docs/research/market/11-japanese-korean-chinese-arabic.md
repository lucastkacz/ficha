# 11 japanese-korean-chinese-arabic
Sources read: 81 (25 App Store review feeds, 16 store pages, 40 articles, threads and data pages). Covered from 2016 to 2026.

How to read the counts: a summarizing model read each page of up to about 50 reviews, so counts are approximate. A review can count under several themes. The 381 AnkiMobile reviews are jp, kr and cn (2 pages each, 100 reviews each), tw (50), sa (22) and eg (9). Feeds: [jp](https://itunes.apple.com/jp/rss/customerreviews/id=373493387/sortby=mostrecent/json), [kr](https://itunes.apple.com/kr/rss/customerreviews/id=373493387/sortby=mostrecent/json), [cn](https://itunes.apple.com/cn/rss/customerreviews/id=373493387/sortby=mostrecent/json), [tw](https://itunes.apple.com/tw/rss/customerreviews/id=373493387/sortby=mostrecent/json), [sa](https://itunes.apple.com/sa/rss/customerreviews/id=373493387/sortby=mostrecent/json), [eg](https://itunes.apple.com/eg/rss/customerreviews/id=373493387/sortby=mostrecent/json), all read 2026-11-05. First pages run 2024 to 2026 for jp, kr and cn, and back to 2017 for tw, sa and eg, [OLD] in part. Second pages, used for widget counts, reach back to 2021.

## Findings

### Which apps, and for what

**Japan: exams drive it.**
- [SAID] Anki reviewers name national exams, the bar exam, university entrance and English vocabulary: about 4 of 50 ([jp feed](https://itunes.apple.com/jp/rss/customerreviews/id=373493387/sortby=mostrecent/json)). Note writers add the 社労士 and 行政書士 exams ([2025-10-12](https://note.com/aokigyoseisyosi/n/ne28883129dae)).
- [CLAIMED] 暗記メーカー makes question sets for school tests and qualifications: 1.9M downloads, 14K ratings at 4.7 ([store](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id1201200202)).
- [CLAIMED] reminDO, a memo app that notifies you when review is due: 18K ratings at 4.6 ([store](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id1058865810)).
- [CLAIMED] Monoxer sells through schools and cram schools, about 4,000 classrooms per the company ([search result, company statements](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20220425/81582.html) [OLD 2022]). A student who built a rival says it is mainly for institutions ([2026-06-16, promotes own app](https://note.com/kuct/n/n0ab1d600104e)).
- [SAID] For ready-made English lists, Q&A answers point to mikan, abceed and 物書堂, and to Quizlet for free ([2025-04-30](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q14314414749), [2024-11-03](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q14306199619)).

**Korea: English vocabulary, assigned by teachers or bought as a course.**
- [CLAIMED] ClassCard: 110K teachers and 2.3M students, 26K ratings at 4.2, free ([store](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1176435331)). [SAID] About 9 of 50 reviews say a school or academy assigns it ([feed, 2026-10 to 11](https://itunes.apple.com/kr/rss/customerreviews/id=1176435331/sortby=mostrecent/json)).
- [CLAIMED] MalhaeVoca: first in Korean education revenue, about $35M cumulative, 92.5% of it from Korea ([Sensor Tower, 2024, sells its data](https://sensortower.com/ko/blog/revenue-of-the-korean-market-education-app-and-MalhaeVoca-reached-all-time-highs)).
- [SAID] Anki is used for vocabulary and kanji, and some reviews mention returning to it after RemNote: 1 of 50 for the return ([kr feed](https://itunes.apple.com/kr/rss/customerreviews/id=373493387/sortby=mostrecent/json)).

**China: 四六级 and 考研 word lists, then Anki for those who want control.**
- [CLAIMED] Store ratings: 百词斩 1.72M, 墨墨 990K, 不背单词 455K ([墨墨](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id888483369); [百词斩](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id557545298) and 不背 from dated snapshots in search results).
- [SAID] V2EX threads recommend 欧路 and 扇贝 next to Anki. Anki had 4 mentions in one 12-reply thread ([2023](https://v2ex.com/t/950830)), and a 25-reply thread says it loses to 欧路 or 扇贝 for words alone ([2024](https://www.v2ex.com/t/1074812)).
- [SAID] Anki also holds 考研 politics question banks and engineering courses ([sspai, 2019, OLD](https://sspai.com/post/57250)).

**Taiwan.** Evidence is thin. Vocabulary is the main named use in the [tw feed](https://itunes.apple.com/tw/rss/customerreviews/id=373493387/sortby=mostrecent/json). About 10 of 50 reviews, mostly 2018 to 2023 [OLD], ask for a Traditional Chinese interface.

**Arabic: religious study is a large use I rarely see in English.**
- [CLAIMED] A Telegram channel with 15.5K subscribers shares Anki decks for "students of knowledge": Quranic vocabulary, tafsir, hadith, fiqh, Arabic grammar, plus TOEFL lists. The page I read never mentions medicine ([channel, read 2026-11-05](https://t.me/s/Anki_arabic?before=29)).
- [SAID] One Arabic blogger also uses Anki for the Quran ([2021, revised 2026](https://www.abdnh.net/anki-intro)).
- [CLAIMED] Quran memorization is mostly recitation tools. Al Jazeera's list names Tarteel, Tasmee and Khatmah, not schedulers ([2026-02-18](https://www.aljazeera.net/tech/2026/2/18/عصر-المصحف-الذكي-تعرف-على-أفضل)).
- [CLAIMED] Quran apps that say they use spaced repetition are tiny: Tathbeet under 1,000 downloads and no ratings ([mwm.ai, read 2026-11-05, US data](https://mwm.ai/apps/app/6757826826)), and a web tool with 2 GitHub stars ([zz](https://github.com/noureddin/zz/)).
- [SAID] المقرئ repeats verses: 34 of 50 reviews give 5 stars, about 12 say it helps memorizing or revising ([sa feed, 2020 to 2026](https://itunes.apple.com/sa/rss/customerreviews/id=1450865704/sortby=mostrecent/json)).

## Evidence against

- **Lock-screen apps are small:** Android installs of 230K to 450K aren't small, and I couldn't read Chinese Android stores at all. Rating counts understate use. [INFERRED] from reach, not reviews.
- **Nobody asks for notifications:** reminDO has 18K ratings built on them, and 墨墨's reminders get praise.
- **They don't pay:** MalhaeVoca's revenue, 墨墨's break-even on word caps alone ([36kr, 2017, OLD](https://www.36kr.com/p/1721882198017)) and reviews saying they bought AnkiMobile to support its maker (cn 1, kr 1 of 50) all go against it.
- **Anki is hated:** some 5-star reviews complain of its interface and stay 5 stars.
- **Local apps' praise:** the latest 50 reviews of 墨墨 fall within about 13 hours and 94% give 5 stars, and 不背单词's within about a day at 84%. Word cap is earned by sharing ([woshipm, 2021](https://www.woshipm.com/evaluating/5111381.html)), and a store comment in a search result alleges reviews traded for it (second-hand). [INFERRED] These feeds may be incentivized. Treat their low complaint counts with care.
- **Local critics sell things:** Japanese "Anki is hard" posts promote the writer's own きおくま; 闪卡词典 and Any to Anki threads are makers' posts.
