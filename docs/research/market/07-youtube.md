### What they praise, criticize and recommend

**Praise** `[SAID]`, 4 of the 11 videos with readable content:
- Free, open source, automates scheduling: Abdaal (2 transcripts); Sung, who says managing it by hand would be a nightmare ([summary](https://opentools.ai/youtube-summary/how-to-use-anki-like-a-pro-by-a-learning-expert)); The Trending Profesora ([summary](https://videohighlight.com/v/F6T1rwbxBGE)).
- Works across subjects, cuts exam stress (Abdaal Masterclass, transcript).
- **Review in spare moments.** Abdaal's Masterclass: cards on the toilet, on the bus, and Anki as the first icon on his phone to replace scrolling Instagram (transcript). Sung, a critic, also approves 3 to 7 minute bursts while commuting or in a queue, 20 to 30 minutes a day (summary). So 2 of 2 who address this like it, always as a session the user opens. The transcript mentions no notifications.

**Criticism** `[SAID]`, counted over the 8 videos with readable criticism: Lampariello; "Why Language Learners Hate Anki"; "Why you shouldn't use flashcards"; Steve Kaufmann; Sung's PRO and PROBLEM videos; "What Everyone Gets Wrong"; Livakivi's 20,000-card stream.

1. **Isolated, out of context, 5 of 8.**
   - Lampariello ([summary](https://opentools.ai/youtube-summary/why-i-dont-use-anki-to-learn-vocabulary-and-why-you-shouldnt-either)).
   - "Why you shouldn't use flashcards" ([LingQ forum](https://forum.lingq.com/t/video-why-you-shouldnt-use-flashcards/29914), June 2020 `[OLD]`).
   - Kaufmann, who says he never used Anki ([transcript](https://www.lingq.com/en/learn-english-online/courses/135380/how-to-review-vocabulary-14384570/), about 2022 `[OLD]`, date inferred).
   - Sung PRO: one fact per card doesn't build connected understanding (summary).
   - "What Everyone Gets Wrong": good for isolated facts, poor for complex ideas ([site's summary](https://youtube-transcript.ai/docs/what-everyone-gets-wrong-about-spaced-repetition), channel unknown).
2. **Review pile and overwhelm, 5 of 8.**
   - Lampariello.
   - "Why Language Learners Hate Anki": about 1,000 reviews within weeks ([Anki forum](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/please-watch-this-video/16395), Jan 2022 `[OLD]`, channel unconfirmed).
   - Sung PRO: hundreds or thousands of cards probably do more harm than good; about 100 to 150 a week is reasonable (summary).
   - Sung PROBLEM: repetitive, time-consuming, demoralizing, diminishing returns ([summary](https://briefread.pages.dev/en/summary/the-problem-with-active-recall-and), Apr 2025).
   - Livakivi: wants to stop daily card quotas to spend time on immersion and output ([summary](https://chattube.io/summary/people-blogs/h7_6B8zjo7Q)).
3. **Hard to start, unpolished, 2 of the 11.** Abdaal: "not very pretty" and unintuitive at first. The Trending Profesora: steep learning curve. An [Ed Tech Lab comparison](https://musichero.ai/blog-best-flashcard-app-a-review-of-anki-quizlet-flashcard-lab-cram-and-brainscape-54068) (2023 `[OLD]`, snippet only) agrees.
4. **Time spent making cards, 1 of 8.** Lampariello: several minutes per card.

Some critics blame how people use Anki more than Anki itself: "What Everyone Gets Wrong" says Anki isn't bad; Sung gives a fix. `[SAID]`

## Evidence against

- **"Anti-Anki videos shape opinion" may be overstated.** The biggest numbers I could see are pro-Anki: Abdaal's 2.3M and 943K, against 60,250 for Livakivi's stop video and 4,891 for the Lampariello interview. Jouzu Juls's Anki tips alone beat the stop video. But I lack views for Lampariello's main video and Sung's Anki videos, and the count is only what stats pages showed.
- **"Out of context" is contested.** Migaku's advice is to keep flashcards and use sentences; Matt vs Japan's titles point the same way. The Substack reply shows a sentence-based 15 minutes a day. Critics who sell an alternative have a stake.
- **"Overwhelm" may be a use problem.** The forum poster's low limits removed it. Abdaal handles overload with limits and a load balancer add-on. Livakivi's 20,000-card stream reads as finishing a goal, not failing at one.
- **Spare-moment review is not proven by this.** Abdaal and Sung speak of opening a session on a commute, not of cards arriving on their own. Sung's own PROBLEM video doubts spaced repetition's value for strong students. I found no independent evidence on widgets or notifications; [one article's claim](https://studycardsai.com/blog/best-flashcard-app-for-iphone) that widgets make it much more effective cites no study and comes from a site selling an AI flashcard tool.
- **The summaries could be wrong.** They're AI-made and warn of errors; one [Anki page](https://summify.io/discover/entities/software/anki/) mixed in a robotics company. Forum samples are tiny and self-selected.
