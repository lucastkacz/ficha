# 13 quizlet-brainscape
Sources read: 45. Covered from 2014 to 2026.

Most "best Quizlet alternative" pages sell something; each is flagged. Store and Trustpilot figures were read on 2026-11-05.

## Findings

### Quizlet's sheet

**Who it's for.** School and college students, and teachers. [CLAIMED] [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quizlet-more-than-flashcards/id546473125) names languages, the MCAT and a history quiz. 60M+ monthly users as of 2021, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quizlet) [OLD, 2021].

**Features and advantages.** Shared sets, Flashcards, Match, Learn, Test, class games, AI notes. In 2026 it bought Coconote, which makes study material from recordings ([THE Journal, 2026-02-13](https://thejournal.com/Articles/2026/02/13/Quizlet-Adds-AI-Powered-Tools-for-Active-Practice-Notetaking.aspx?p=1)), and added a ChatGPT integration ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quizlet)). [CLAIMED] Its ready-made decks are its key value, one commenter says ([HN, 2022](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=32633110)) [SAID, 1] [OLD, 2022]. Even Brainscape concedes its game-like study is strong ([vendor page, 2026](https://www.brainscape.com/academy/brainscape-vs-quizlet/)) [CLAIMED].

**Complaints, with counts.**
- Trustpilot: 1.4/5 from 664 reviews, 84% one-star, 88 in the last 12 months ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/quizlet.com)). Quizlet hasn't recently invited reviews, so the sample leans angry. [SAID]
- In the 40 newest reviews, May to Nov 2026, my rough counts: paywall about 18, billing or charges after cancelling about 12, support about 8, ads about 6, bugs about 7 ([page 1](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/quizlet.com), [page 2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/quizlet.com?page=2)). Reviews can hold several themes. [SAID]
- SmartCustomer (formerly Sitejabber): 3.1/5 from 33; of 20 visible reviews, 8 on paywalls, 4 on ads and emails, 7 positive ([page](https://www.smartcustomer.com/reviews/quizlet.com), Nov 2019 to Sep 2024) [SAID] [OLD for most].
- Across five flashcard apps' 1-to-3-star reviews, 26% say good features got paywalled and 17% say free features were removed. Quizlet is called the most resented; the sample size is undisclosed and there are no per-app figures ([Unstar, 2026-06-24](https://unstar.app/blog/quizlet-anki-brainscape-knowt-studysmarter-flashcard-study-apps-ranked-2026), sells review analysis) [CLAIMED].
- User-made sets contain errors: 1 SmartCustomer review, and an Anki forum reply calling Quizlet cards "hit-or-miss" ([2024](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/is-there-a-way-to-mass-import-a-quizlet-library-into-anki/43711)) [SAID] [OLD, 2024].
- Accessibility: one blind-user App Store review says it has never been accessible ([2022, same listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quizlet-more-than-flashcards/id546473125)) [SAID, 1] [OLD, 2022].

**Price and how it changed.**
- Free: a limited number of Learn rounds per set and one practice test per set, per [Quizlet's help centre](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360030986971-Studying-with-Learn), seen only as a search snippet [CLAIMED]. Blogs say "daily caps" instead ([Nibble](https://nibble-app.com/blog/quizlet-cost), [FrontDeskReview](https://frontdeskreview.com/software/ai-study-tools/quizlet/), 2026). No source gave a current number; the sources conflict.
- Plus: $7.99/month or $35.99/year, with 3 practice tests, 20 Learn rounds and 3 solutions a month, as quoted from Quizlet's page on 2026-09-08 ([Mindomax](https://www.mindomax.com/quizlet-plus-cost), sells a rival). The help centre confirms "monthly usage limits" ([snippet](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041181691-Subscribing-to-Quizlet)). [CLAIMED]
- Plus Unlimited: $9.99/month or $44.99/year, no caps. [CLAIMED] iOS lists $9.99 and $44.99 plans ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quizlet-more-than-flashcards/id546473125)).
- Several 2026 blogs call Plus unlimited ([Nibble](https://nibble-app.com/blog/quizlet-cost), a [tutoring site](https://www.myengineeringbuddy.com/blog/quizlet-reviews-alternatives-pricing-offerings/)), and [Headway, 2026-09-17](https://makeheadway.com/blog/quizlet-review/) still lists Gravity and Q-Chat as current. These blogs look stale. [INFERRED]
- History: premium tier since 2017 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quizlet)). Plus was $7.99/$35.99 in 2022 and is the same now, but it now has monthly caps, with a new tier above it. So what Plus buys seems to have shrunk while its price held. [INFERRED from 2022 and 2026 sources; I found no dated announcement of the split]
- Cancelling doesn't refund the current period ([Nibble](https://nibble-app.com/blog/quizlet-cost)) [CLAIMED]. I found no FTC action or lawsuit.

**Why people move to it and leave it.** To it: ready-made sets, teachers assigning it, games, friendlier than Anki (a [student paper](https://pawebpage.com/3269/opinions/quizlets-paywall-proves-that-students-are-its-last-priority) finds Anki's interface confusing, [OLD, 2022]; a [2026 thread](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1qj2vs5/snapshots/2026-01-21T22%3A00%3A23.593261Z) praises Quizlet's interface and Match, second-hand). [SAID] Leaving: see the paywall section.

**Notifications, widgets, lock screen, menu bar, watch.**
- Push notifications include study reminders, recommendations and updates. Spaced-repetition reviews, available only on the website, may bring a daily reminder at 10 a.m. local time ([notifications](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043951051-Changing-your-notification-preferences), [spaced repetition](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/48324742264077-Studying-with-Spaced-Repetition), help-centre snippets, undated) [CLAIMED].
- Widget: the App Store listing doesn't mention one, and the help-centre search found no article. A single low-quality [blog](https://jitsibb.subinsb.com/your-study-routine-best-ios-d1ia.html) describes one; I don't trust it. Watch and lock screen: no evidence of either. Menu bar: no evidence. [INFERRED: absence, not proof]
- Reception: I found no one praising or complaining about its reminders. SmartCustomer reviews do complain of heavy email after sign-up, among its 4 on ads and emails. [SAID]

**First sentences.** App Store: "Whatever you're studying, a language, the MCAT, or next week's history quiz" (it continues; cut for the quote limit). Own site: unread, see Blockers.

**Alive?** Yes. Version 10.59, about five days before reading, and 26 versions since May 4 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quizlet-more-than-flashcards/id546473125)). Revenue reported at $139M in June 2025 ([Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/quizlet.com), snippet, citing Yahoo Finance) [CLAIMED, unverified].

### Quizlet's paywall moves and how they landed

**The moves.**
- From 2022-08-01: Learn and Test capped at five rounds and one test per set; Write and Spell folded into Learn; Gravity removed; only Flashcards and Match stay free ([The Gator, 2022-11-15](https://bmgator.org/33901/news/changes-to-quizlet-cause-frustration-among-students/), [The Catalyst, 2022-09-23](https://thendbcatalyst.com/10077/news/quizlet-moves-many-popular-features-to-payment-plan)). Slader's solutions went paid despite a 2021 promise to keep them free (The Catalyst). 7 of 7 student papers agree on the core. [OLD, 2022]
- Quizlet's words: changes may "create disruptions in study routines" ([Eagle's Eye, 2022-11-07](https://ehseagleseye.com/6095/features/quizlet-from-free-to-paid)), and Flashcards "are (and will always be!) free" ([Spartan Shield, 2022-09-07](https://spartanshield.org/33765/opinion/new-year-new-frustrations-quizlet-switches-to-payment-based-plans/)). [CLAIMED] [OLD, 2022]
- June 2025: Q-Chat retired ([Quizgecko](https://quizgecko.com/blog/best-q-chat-alternative), sells a rival, snippet; one [App Store review, 2025-07-29](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/quizlet-more-than-flashcards/id546473125) notes it too). [CLAIMED]
- Some blogs claim further cuts in late 2025; I couldn't verify a date. Trustpilot reviewers in 2026 complain of locked full sets, tests and image uploads, and that automatic flashcard playback was removed. [SAID, roughly 8 of 20]

**The reception.**
- Student press, autumn 2022: all 7 papers I read report frustration or argue against it. Their recurring points: students can't pay (about 4), the free modes are the ones that don't work for them (about 3), it broke study habits they'd built (1, and Quizlet's own admission). The [WA Ghostwriter, 2022-10-06](https://waghostwriter.com/?p=84703) reports online calls to boycott. [SAID] [OLD, 2022]
- Workarounds: duplicate sets (WA Ghostwriter), a Gravity URL ([The Page, 2022-10-13](https://pawebpage.com/3269/opinions/quizlets-paywall-proves-that-students-are-its-last-priority)), and teacher-assigned sets, which stay unlimited (The Page, The Catalyst). [SAID] [OLD, 2022]
- It still hurts: in 2026 about 18 of 40 recent Trustpilot reviews are about the paywall. [SAID]

### Where those who left went

No source measures this. What follows is who gets named.

- **Student papers, 2022** (7 read): Knowt 2 (both in reader comments), Anki/AnkiDroid 2 (one dismissive), Kahoot 2, RemNote 1, Memrise 1, Quizizz 1, Course Hero 1. Two papers report only "a different website" or none. Spartan Shield reports people paying, adapting or stopping, not moving. [SAID] [OLD, 2022]
- **Knowt: the like-for-like move.** The 2 roundups I read in full, and several more in snippets, call it the closest replacement, for its free Learn and Quizlet import ([Heuristica](https://www.heuristi.ca/posts/quizlet-alternatives), [Fiveable](https://fiveable.me/best-quizlet-alternatives.md), both 2026, both sell something). App Store: 4.7 from 11K, "7 million students," a review titled "Goodbye Quizlet, hello Knowt!!!" ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6463744184)) [CLAIMED/SAID]. Visits 5.3M in May 2026, up from 2.9M in January ([ToolMage snippet](https://www.toolmage.com/de/compare/knowt-vs-studydrive/)), against about 65M a month for Quizlet, so roughly a twelfth. [INFERRED, mixed estimates]
- **Knowt repeats the pattern.** Features moving behind Ultra is a "recurring complaint," with one reviewer contesting the Quizlet-alternative label ([Headway, 2026-09-08](https://makeheadway.com/blog/knowt-review/), sells an app). [SAID, second-hand]
- **Anki: the move for retention.** In a [r/GCSE thread, 2026-01-21](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1qj2vs5/snapshots/2026-01-21T22%3A00%3A23.593261Z), about 13 of 17 captured comments prefer Anki; Gizmo 1, Knowt 1 (second-hand, third-party snapshot). An Anki-forum user a week in calls it much better and wants to move a whole library ([2024](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/is-there-a-way-to-mass-import-a-quizlet-library-into-anki/43711)) [OLD, 2024]. Anki tops [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/quizlet/)'s list with 725 likes, Notion 700, Duolingo 366, though likes pile up over years and aren't moves. [SAID]
- **Moving out is hard.** Importer add-ons break, as captchas block them; plain text export is the simple route ([Anki forum, 2021 on](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/import-from-quizlet/14377)) [SAID, at least 6 threads by title] [OLD for most].
- **Chatbots, perhaps most of all.** Estimated visits fell 29%, from 1.1B to 782.7M, May 2025 to April 2026 against the prior year, while Quizlet still held 95.55% of its category and chatbots grew 91% ([PPC Land, 2026-09-03](https://ppc.land/chegg-loses-61-of-web-visits-as-chatbots-gain-90-82-yoy/), on OneLittleWeb, window inferred by the article's method). So no rival study app took that traffic; general AI tools are a suggested cause, not a shown one. [INFERRED]

### Brainscape's sheet

**Who it's for.** Serious long-term learners, exam takers (NCLEX, CFA, WSET), language learners, plus schools and companies ([site](https://www.brainscape.com/)). [CLAIMED]

**Features and advantages.** Confidence-Based Repetition on a 1-to-5 scale, certified decks vetted by experts, AI card creation from files, sharing, creators can sell decks, and an ad-free free plan ([site](https://www.brainscape.com/), [vs Quizlet](https://www.brainscape.com/academy/brainscape-vs-quizlet/)) [CLAIMED]. Reviewers praise ease of use, the confidence rating and the decks, mainly for languages and exams ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/brainscape.com)) [SAID]. Its own page admits its scheduling "isn't quite so prescriptive" as Anki's, and that user-made decks vary ([vs Anki](https://brainscape.com/academy/brainscape-vs-anki)) [CLAIMED].

**Complaints, with counts.**
- Trustpilot overall is strong: 4.7 from 602, 86% five-star, 190 in the last 12 months, replies to 100% of negative reviews ([page](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/brainscape.com)). I didn't check whether it invites reviews.
- Of 17 one- and two-star reviews shown, 2021 to Oct 2026: paywall or price about 10, support 6, content quality 4, bugs 3, scheduling 2 ([filtered](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/brainscape.com?stars=1&stars=2)). Overlap with the newest list is possible. [SAID]
- Scheduling: highly rated cards vanish for months, 2 reviewers. [SAID]
- Missing: text-to-speech promised for 2025 ([App Store review, 2025-12-23](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brainscape-study-flashcards/id442415567)), test mode and typed answers (2 on Trustpilot). [SAID]
- Lost purchases after an app consolidation ([App Store review, 2023](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brainscape-study-flashcards/id442415567)) [SAID, 1] [OLD, 2023].
- Thin content for languages: few expert classes, meanings without grammar ([FluentU, 2025-02](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/reviews/brainscape/), sells a language course) [CLAIMED].

**Price and how it changed.**
- Basic, free: unlimited text cards, importing, a few hundred AI cards, spaced repetition, 2,500 preloaded cards ([pricing](https://www.brainscape.com/pricing)). Pro: images and sound on cards, reverse cards, private decks, unlimited AI and certified decks. [CLAIMED]
- Pro is $7.99/month billed yearly on the web. iOS: $19.99/month, $59.99 per six months, $95.99/year, $199.99 lifetime ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brainscape-study-flashcards/id442415567), [Mindomax, 2026-09-11](https://www.mindomax.com/brainscape-vs-anki), sells a rival). [CLAIMED]
- Almost the same in [FluentU, 2025](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/reviews/brainscape/), so price looks stable; free access to others' decks went from the first two decks per class to the 2,500-card allowance. [INFERRED]
- What people say: "costly" is G2's summarised complaint (about 330 reviews, 4.6/5, [snippet](https://www.g2.com/products/brainscape)) [SAID, second-hand]. Media requires a plan, one [Student Doctor](https://forums.studentdoctor.net/threads/how-to-get-around-paying-anki-for-ios.1512842) poster notes (snippet) [SAID, 1]. Brainscape replies on Trustpilot that it doesn't paywall previously free features; a low-star reviewer says otherwise. [CLAIMED vs SAID]

**Why people move to it and leave it.** To it: cleaner than Anki, mastery percentage ([App Store review](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brainscape-study-flashcards/id442415567), 2022 [OLD]), expert decks. Leaving: the paywall, no text-to-speech, scheduling. AlternativeTo points to Anki (725) and Memrise (200) ([page](https://alternativeto.net/software/brainscape/)). I found no thread about a mass move away. [INFERRED]

**Notifications, widgets, lock screen, menu bar, watch.** Streak-reminder push notifications with a chosen time, launched Oct 2023 as a long-requested feature; Brainscape calls them "gentle" nags ([page](https://www.brainscape.com/academy/study-reminders/), [news](https://www.brainscape.com/academy/news)) [CLAIMED]. The App Store's compatibility lists iPhone, iPad and Vision, no Watch or Mac, and the listing mentions no widget ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brainscape-study-flashcards/id442415567)). No reception found.

**First sentences.** Site: "Use AI to find or make flashcards from any source." under "The ultimate study weapon." App Store: "Learn faster and remember longer with Brainscape, the AI flashcard app" (it continues).

**Alive?** Yes. Version 4.20261002, Oct 19; steady news through Oct 2026, Flashcard Copilot in Jan 2026 ([news](https://www.brainscape.com/academy/news), dates inferred there).

## Evidence against

- **The anger isn't in the store ratings.** Quizlet is 4.8 from 1.1M on the App Store, and about 4.3 from 765K on Google Play ([tutoring site](https://www.myengineeringbuddy.com/blog/quizlet-reviews-alternatives-pricing-offerings/), second-hand). Trustpilot's 664 are a sliver of that and self-selected.
- **Paywall as a success.** Reported revenue $80M (Oct 2024 estimate) to $139M (June 2025) ([Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/quizlet.com), snippet, unverified and possibly not comparable). Its 95.55% category share suggests few rivals gained.
- **Some accepted it.** Two students in 2022 papers accepted that Quizlet needs revenue, one of whom paid ([Spartan Shield](https://spartanshield.org/33765/opinion/new-year-new-frustrations-quizlet-switches-to-payment-based-plans/), [Eagle's Eye](https://ehseagleseye.com/6095/features/quizlet-from-free-to-paid)). [OLD, 2022]
- **Traffic fell, but the cause isn't shown.** The estimates are panel-based, and consumer edtech overall fell 5% in another dataset (PPC Land).
- **Anki isn't an easy landing.** Roundups repeat its steep curve; one 2022 student paper dismissed it as confusing; its iOS app costs $24.99.
- **Brainscape's paywall complaints may be a minority.** 86% five-star, with low-star reviews spread over five years.
