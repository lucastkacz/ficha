### What people who want a native app ask for

- **The richest record is one thread.** [MPU's "Mac/iOS flashcards app"](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/mac-ios-flashcards-app/17508) (36 posts, 2020-2024, `[OLD]`): the asker wants to enter cards on the Mac and study on iOS. Rough recommendations `[SAID]`: Anki about 4-5 people, Studies 2-3, Mochi 2-3, Quizlet 1, Fresh Cards 1, Brainscape 1, GoodNotes 1. Anki draws "dated" and "clunky"; others say theming fixes it. Privacy: 0 mentions. iCloud: 1. A [second thread](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/apps-to-learn-names/9105) (22 posts `[OLD]`) repeats those.
- **Recommended by name, in what I read:** Anki leads; Studies and Mochi follow; Fresh Cards trails. [Sweet Setup](https://thesweetsetup.com/apps/best-flash-card-study-app-ios/) picked Studies in 2017 `[OLD]`, its links look affiliate. No forum recommended AlgoApp, Noji, Flashcard Hero, NKO, Flashcards World or Zorbi.- **Requests for a native Anki are few.** 5 Anki forum threads read across 2021-2025, 32 posts, plus one from 2022 unread: a [dated look](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-needs-a-more-modern-version-for-macbook/16005), [AnkiMobile on Mac](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/please-make-ankimobile-flashcards-available-to-run-on-m1-macs/10176) ([2](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-on-the-mac-app-store/30538), [3](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-on-macos/35303)) `[OLD]`, [sandboxing through the Mac App Store](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/add-anki-to-mac-app-store/62520) (2025, 17 posts). Its developer `[SAID]` declines: audio crashes on Mac, a free desktop app exists, and the license doesn't fit the store.
- **Makers, not users, voice the wish list most.** `[INFERRED]` Of 12 newcomers from 2024-2026 (7 in the table, plus [sumi](https://edenroye.github.io/sumi/anki-alternative-ios.html), Meadow Study by snippet, [Cardcore](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6739949798), Cards.my, Amgi), about 9 pitch FSRS, 9 Anki import, all 12 iCloud or local-only, 4 a one-time price alone, 3 a lifetime option beside a subscription. ### Do they care?

- **One-time price: yes, most visibly.** `[SAID]` Praised in store reviews of Flashcards Deluxe and Fresh Cards (some of the few shown) and AnkiMobile (1). Removal of a lifetime option drew a complaint at NKO. [About 8 of 55](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1573585542/anki-pro-flashcards-learning/reviews) Noji reviews object to its price, some against AnkiMobile's one payment.
- **No account or local storage: some.** `[SAID]` Several commenters praise it on [FlashCard's launch](https://www.producthunt.com/products/flashcard) (2026); one asks whether a crash could lose everything.
- **iCloud: as sync, rarely as privacy.** Reviewers praise iCloud when it works (Studies, Fresh Cards, Flashcard Hero); failures are noted too. One seller [says on HN](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=Manabi%20Reader%20iCloud%20privacy%20customers&tags=comment&hitsPerPage=10) his customers value data in their own iCloud (May 2025, sells it).
- **Privacy as such: almost absent.** 0 mentions in MPU's 36 posts; none in about 50 visible store reviews, as summarized. [Privacy Guides' thread](https://discuss.privacyguides.net/t/student-application-for-making-flash-cards/21230) (8 posts, 2024 `[OLD]`) has 1 person wary of Anki's sync and no Apple app. Against this, Fresh Cards, Wokabulary and Truly Simple advertise Data Not Collected, while top-rated Noji labels tracking.

## Evidence against

- **Against "native matters":** MPU posters also recommend Quizlet, web-only, and Mochi for polish, not for being native. One Anki forum poster [prefers the plain look](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-needs-a-more-modern-version-for-macbook/16005). Anki reportedly ships [natively on Apple Silicon](https://rosettacheck.com/apps/net.ankiweb.dtop) since 25.09 (tracker, snippet), though [26.09 drew Mac lag posts](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/26-09-performance-issues/71033) (snippet).
- **Against "one-time wins":** the two largest apps here sell subscriptions and rate 4.6-4.7; I can't say how many buy.- **Against "iCloud is reliable":** complaints at Flashcard Hero, Studies (data corruption, 2019 `[OLD]`), and Fresh Cards' own notes on iCloud fixes.
- **Against my own counts:** forum numbers are small and old, store pages show few reviews, and r/macapps, where privacy talk might be stronger, went unread.
