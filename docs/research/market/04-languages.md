# 04 languages
Sources read: 47 through WebFetch, plus about 30 seen only as search results. Covered from 2013 to 2026.

Method note: WebFetch returns a small model's reading of each page, so counts are rough and quotes should be checked before being reused. Each forum draws its own crowd: LingQ sells reading, Bunpro and WaniKani sell paid SRS. `[SNIPPET]` means search result only, not opened.

## Findings

### How they use Anki and rivals, what they value, why they quit

**Usual path: a premade starter deck, then own cards from reading.** `[SAID]` Frequent advice in a [Bunpro thread](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/should-i-use-bunpro-for-vocabulary-or-anki-or-both/127282) (about 12 people, Apr 2025). `[CLAIMED]` [Refold's guide](https://refold.la/roadmap/library/learning-words-with-anki) (undated; it sells decks) says the same: 5 to 10 new cards a day, study at 25 to 30% of learning time. [TheMoeWay's guide](https://learnjapanese.moe/mining/) (undated) starts from Kaishi 1.5k, then desktop mining.

**Anki is valued for control.** `[SAID]` In [Ditching Anki for Bunpro?](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/ditching-anki-for-bunpro/129970) (13 posters, May to Jul 2025), those staying cite editing cards, images, leech suspension, the browser and mining. Speed too: one poster has [reviews under 10 seconds with FSRS](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/is-vocab-study-on-bunpro-actually-good/81224) `[OLD 2024]`. Many run two tools: Bunpro for JLPT lists, Anki for words met in the wild.

**Daily time among those who keep going is modest.** `[SAID]` 15 minutes ([LingQ](https://forum.lingq.com/t/use-anki-or-not/380455), `[OLD 2024]`, one poster suggests under 10% of study time), 10 to 20 ([LingQ](https://forum.lingq.com/t/is-anki-a-necessity/36860), `[OLD 2023]`), 20 to 30 for about 1,000 cards ([12-year user](https://momomori-eki.net/2020/08/04/twelve-years-of-anki/), `[OLD 2020]`), one hour ([Chinese-Forums](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/62801-how-can-i-scale-my-vocab-learning/), `[OLD 2023]`).

**Why they quit, by rough frequency across about 12 threads:**
1. **Review pile and falling behind (in about 8 threads).** `[SAID]` [LingQ 2021](https://forum.lingq.com/t/do-anki-flash-cards-work/32254) `[OLD]` (13 posters): volume overwhelming, breaks hard to recover from. [LingQ 2024-25](https://forum.lingq.com/t/use-anki-or-not/380455) `[OLD 2024]`: 4 use it, 4 or 5 stopped. One learner still had [110 reviews a day](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/62801-how-can-i-scale-my-vocab-learning/) six months after stopping new cards `[OLD 2023]`. Same pain in paid SRS: [133 WaniKani reviews, nearly an hour](https://community.wanikani.com/t/painful-reviews/75512) (5 people, Aug 2026). The 12-year user reports backlogs after months-long lapses and regrets deleting a deck to restart.
2. **Time spent making cards (about 5 threads).** `[SAID]` [LingQ 2021](https://forum.lingq.com/t/do-anki-flash-cards-work/32254) `[OLD]`, [Mochi thread](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/if-you-dont-like-anki-consider-giving-mochi-a-try/59955) `[OLD 2023]`, [Ditching Anki](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/ditching-anki-for-bunpro/129970) (2025).
3. **Setup and interface (about 5).** `[SAID]` A 12-year user says [setup stops most students](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/64243-whats-better-than-pleco-and-anki-these-days/), who go back to Quizlet or paper (16 members, Jan to Apr 2026). [About 5 of 20 posts on WaniKani](https://community.wanikani.com/t/whats-wanikani-actually-good-for/69253) mention Anki, mostly negatively (Feb 2025).
4. **Doesn't transfer to real use (about 4).** `[SAID]` Words learned in sentences not recognized elsewhere ([LingQ 2021](https://forum.lingq.com/t/do-anki-flash-cards-work/32254) `[OLD]`); a 5-year user names [an illusion of knowing and stubborn reviews of words never met again](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/63123-reflections-on-5-years-of-daily-anki/) `[OLD 2023]`.
5. **Reading seen as a better use of the time.** `[SAID]` [LingQ, Jan 2023](https://forum.lingq.com/t/is-anki-a-necessity/36860) `[OLD]`: about 8 of 15 say not necessary, about 7 useful early or in limited ways. Several say flashcards fade with level; [no one fully quit by level](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/53655-when-did-you-stop-using-plecoankiflashcards/) of 7 posters there `[OLD 2017]`, most trimmed.
6. **Self-grading too easy to cheat.** `[SAID]` Several of 14 in [WaniKani vs Anki](https://community.wanikani.com/t/wanikani-vs-anki/57819) `[OLD 2022]`.

[language-learners.org](https://forum.language-learners.org/viewtopic.php?t=8566&start=10) threads repeat the time sink, burnout and "forgot as much as learned" `[SNIPPET]` `[OLD 2018-23]`; I couldn't count them.

### Small moments of the day: do they want it, what did they try, did it work?

**Spreading reviews through the day is common but not the majority.** `[SAID]` [WaniKani poll-like thread](https://community.wanikani.com/t/do-you-do-reviews-as-they-become-available-or-do-you-a-batch-of-reviews-at-a-specific-time-each-day/49007) `[OLD 2021]`, 18 posters: 7 as available or in small bursts, 6 at 2 or 3 fixed times, 3 one batch, 2 mixed. Reasons for bursts: smaller piles, one says better memory. Reasons against: work, and not wanting it to eat the whole day. Japanese Level Up authors and readers describe commute reviews and 3-minute spurts `[SNIPPET]` `[OLD 2013-16]`, see [catching up](https://japaneselevelup.com/catching-up-anki-reviews-slowly-all-at-once/). LingQ posters list [short gaps in the day](https://forum.lingq.com/t/flashcards-do-you-use-them/21235) `[OLD 2015]`; one Chinese-Forums poster says [flashcards suit downtime](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/63123-reflections-on-5-years-of-daily-anki/) `[OLD 2024]`. On language-learners.org, [one poster ruled out Speakly](https://forum.language-learners.org/viewtopic.php?t=16410) for work breaks and queues because it demands speaking aloud `[SNIPPET]`.

**Asked-for widgets are mostly counts and streaks, not study.** `[SAID]`
- [Bunpro widget thread](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/does-bunpro-have-a-widget-for-its-app-if-not-can-we-get-one-c/72269) `[OLD 2023]`: review count, streak, vocab search. No staff reply.
- [AnkiMobile widget](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-widget/32633?page=2) (4 posters, Dec 2025 to Oct 2026): heat map, stats, a logo reminder. No developer reply; no one says they study languages.
- [AnkiMobile notifications](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/functional-notifications-are-essential-for-ankimobile/66148) (11 posts, Aug 2025 to Aug 2026): due alerts; the developer says partly on the todo list. One reply says multilingual power users always have cards due, so they may not need them.
- Anki's notifications show counts, and I found no native one showing the card itself `[SNIPPET]`, see [notification options](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/notification-options/50544).

**Tries at showing the words themselves:**
- [Watch or lock-screen for hard items](https://community.wanikani.com/t/improving-with-critical-items-with-a-watch-app-widget-or-lock-screen-app/17352) `[OLD 2017]`, 4 people: no working watch or lock-screen tool confirmed; a web card page and wall notes were what got used.
- [JLPT word widget](https://community.wanikani.com/t/jlpt-word-of-the-day-app/65143) `[OLD 2024]`, 5 people: replies are about availability; no one says it helped.
- [KanjiMado](https://community.wanikani.com/t/iphone-widgets-for-wanikani-i-built-an-app-kanjimado-that-lets-you-place-a-widget-on-your-home-screen-showing-your-critical-wanikani-review-items/73804) (5 posters, Mar 2026): warm reactions; one, after a day, likes seeing the words pop up. Others ask to hide the reading and meaning, so they can test themselves `[INFERRED]`.
- [Dumpling Chinese](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/64510-i-built-an-hsk-30-vocabulary-app-with-lock-screen-widgets-%E2%80%94-looking-for-feedback/) (Jun to Oct 2026, 2 non-developer repliers): one praises the widget, one plans to buy the annual plan. No results.
- [Lingo Widget](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lingo-widget-daily-vocabulary/id6740177041): 4.7 from 102 ratings. Reviews like quick practice; one says it suits beginners, and an advanced learner can't hide the pronunciation.
- `[CLAIMED]` [Wordify's blog](https://wordify.it/blog/lock-screen-widgets-that-teach) (Mar 2026, sells one) says glances add up to knowledge, citing no study.

**Did it work? Across about 8 threads and store pages, I found no learner reporting months of results from a widget or lock screen.** `[INFERRED]` Enthusiasm is real but early. Research is thin and mostly measures use, not memory:
- [WaitChatter](https://news.mit.edu/2015/learn-language-while-you-text-0514) `[OLD 2015]`: about four words a day in a two-week pilot; 20 people and 57 words per the [paper](https://sls.csail.mit.edu/publications/2015/Cai_CHI-2015.pdf) `[SNIPPET]`; no retention figure found, and the authors say it won't bring fluency.
- [UnlockLearning](https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid:dd00f355-253c-44a0-bf87-2987295eaeb1) `[OLD 2021]` `[SNIPPET]`: three-week field study; tasks at unlock and in notifications got more answers than a normal app. That's engagement, not retention. It also says Dearman and Truong's [live wallpaper](https://bibtex.github.io/CHI-2012-DearmanT.html) `[OLD 2012]` showed knowledge gain with no standard-app control.

**Notifications can become the complaint.** `[SAID]` About 6 of 10 threads from one search on [LingQ's forum](https://forum.lingq.com/t/bombarded-with-popup-notifications/495147) object to popups and streak nags surviving settings `[SNIPPET]`. These are about streaks and coins, not cards.

### Words alone or in sentences?

**In what they preach, the sentence wins and the bare word is suspect. In what they do, the word often stays the front and the sentence moves to a fallback.**

- **Guides favor sentences.** `[CLAIMED]` [Tatsumoto](https://tatsumoto-ren.github.io/blog/discussing-various-card-templates) `[OLD 2021]`: word cards about 15% of the total, mainly concrete nouns, and targeted sentence cards best; plain sentence cards take about 20 seconds. [Clozemaster's blog](https://www.clozemaster.com/blog/problem-with-single-word-flashcards-context-critical-in-language-learning/) `[OLD 2018]` argues the same but sells cloze and gives little direct comparison.
- **Practice is split and hybrid.** `[SAID]` [WaniKani card types](https://community.wanikani.com/t/lets-discuss-flashcard-types/50144) `[OLD 2021]`, 8 posters: 4 word on the front, 4 sentence or hybrid, of whom 2 admit they rarely read the sentence. Kaishi shows word and sentence together `[SNIPPET]`. [A blogger](https://mikumino.neocities.org/blog/deciding-on-a-card-type) `[OLD 2023]` chose it so eyes can skip the sentence, keeping it as a fallback.
- **Long-term users drift to both.** `[SAID]` The 12-year user found words alone left usage unclear, clozes alone left meanings unclear, and keeps both. The [5-year user](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/63123-reflections-on-5-years-of-daily-anki/) moves to sentences for multi-use verbs and adjectives.
- **Against the bare word.** `[SAID]` [Bunpro 2025](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/should-i-use-bunpro-for-vocabulary-or-anki-or-both/127282): several say it gives false understanding. [Bunpro 2024](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/is-vocab-study-on-bunpro-actually-good/81224) `[OLD]`: 4 posts for sentences, 1 for words. A native speaker says [homophones and synonyms](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/53655-when-did-you-stop-using-plecoankiflashcards/) make word testing senseless `[OLD 2017]`.
- **For the bare word.** `[SAID]` Speed is the main reason. On [LingQ 2021](https://forum.lingq.com/t/do-anki-flash-cards-work/32254) `[OLD]`, one calls sentence learning a waste of time. On language-learners.org, [two threads](https://forum.language-learners.org/viewtopic.php?t=14729) show no consensus, with one poster moving to words to save time and another noting a phrase gives only one use of a word `[SNIPPET]`.
- **Chunks in between.** `[SAID]` Phrases recalled better ([LingQ 2023](https://forum.lingq.com/t/is-anki-a-necessity/36860) `[OLD]`); phrases once word cards get too easy ([Chinese-Forums 2017](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/53655-when-did-you-stop-using-plecoankiflashcards/) `[OLD]`).
- **Research doesn't settle it.** Elgort (2011) `[OLD]` `[SNIPPET]` found [deliberate flashcard learning](https://eurosla31.dryfta.com/program-schedule/program/detail/71/session-3h) builds implicit and explicit knowledge. Small classroom studies conflict: [isolation better](https://ejournal.uin-malang.ac.id/index.php/humbud/article/view/1460) in one, context better in another `[SNIPPET]`.

### Needs they feel Anki leaves unserved
1. **Coming back after a break, and a load that shrinks** (about 8 threads). "You never actually graduate from words."
2. **Cards without the labor** (about 5). jpdb's [known-word tracking across decks](https://community.wanikani.com/t/getting-started-with-jpbd/58352) `[OLD 2022]` and Bunpro's ready sentences are what movers praise.
3. **Working without setup** (about 5).
4. **Forced honesty**, by typing or production (about 3 threads).
5. **Trustworthy native audio.** [Bunpro, Sep 2026](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/pitch-accent-and-anki/220200): 4 of 10 posters on audio, TTS distrusted. One request for [repeat playback](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/how-anki-handles-video-audio-playback/67137) `[SNIPPET]`; [a macOS audio break](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-needs-to-just-work/67116) (1 poster, 5 related threads, Oct 2025).
6. **Language tools built in:** dictionary and tone entry (1 poster, [2026](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/64243-whats-better-than-pleco-and-anki-these-days/)), furigana (Mochi thread).
7. **Reminders and home-screen presence** (2 Anki threads, 15 or so posts), mostly stats and alerts.
8. **Less anxiety:** the 12-year user hides due counts and stats.

## Evidence against
- **Quitting over load:** many stay happily. Some blame [burnout on the learner, not the tool](https://forum.language-learners.org/viewtopic.php?t=18949) `[SNIPPET]`. Advice to cap new cards fixes it for some; the [12-year user](https://momomori-eki.net/2020/08/04/twelve-years-of-anki/) handles 1,000 cards in under 30 minutes. One 5-year user finds [the routine enjoyable](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/63123-reflections-on-5-years-of-daily-anki/).
- **Setup barrier:** a 2026 poster calls the technical barrier [basically a myth](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/64243-whats-better-than-pleco-and-anki-these-days/); another makes all cards through the normal interface.
- **Small moments:** 9 of 18 in the WaniKani thread prefer fixed times or one batch, and Refold advises a consistent time and place. The LingQ crowd wants less flashcard time at all, not spread-out flashcards. A word-of-the-day audience may not overlap with the Anki audience `[INFERRED]`.
- **"Widgets are loved":** warm replies come from small threads (2 to 5 people) that a developer opened, and nothing shows retention.
- **"Sentences win":** see Elgort above. Half the WaniKani posters keep the word on the front, and some with sentence fronts skip them.
- **"Subscriptions are rejected":** Bunpro's and WaniKani's forums hold many satisfied payers, and these forums select for them. A 2026 guest [plans to subscribe annually](https://www.chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/64510-i-built-an-hsk-30-vocabulary-app-with-lock-screen-widgets-%E2%80%94-looking-for-feedback/) to a widget app.
