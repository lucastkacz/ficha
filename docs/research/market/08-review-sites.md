### What the independent ones praise and criticize
Only a few, and thin:
- **Lifehacker** (Oct 2025) [SAID]: seven picks, judged on fast card-making and Leitner spacing. Anki is not among them. It favors Cram's plainness and criticizes NotebookLM for having no Leitner-style spacing.
- **Lifelong Learning Club** (Mar 2026) [SAID]: the visible part reports Anki working for the author and faults AI-made cards. The comparison is paid; only the free part was read.
- **All Language Resources on [WordBit](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/wordbit/)** (May 2024; site discloses affiliate income) [SAID]: 3.0 of 5. The lock-screen overlay exposes you to words well, but it gets in the way of urgent phone tasks, and its ads are easy to tap by accident.
- **[Ubyssey](https://ubyssey.ca/research/tech-flashcard-apps/)** [OLD 2016] and **[College Info Geek](https://collegeinfogeek.com/?p=20840)** [OLD 2022; affiliate]: Quizlet and Anki on top respectively.

Unstar is closest to data: it analyzed 1–3 star store reviews and reports shares of paywalled features 26%, Anki's iOS price 20%, removed features 17%, sync failures 15%, AI or shared-set accuracy 12% [CLAIMED; sample size not stated in what I read; Unstar sells this analysis].

Across all 31, mostly rival-written, so these are repeated claims more than tests [INFERRED: rough counts]:
- **Anki:** praised for its scheduler (about 15) and shared decks (about 11). Criticized for learning curve or setup (about 17), dated interface (about 17), the $24.99 iOS price (about 13), no AI card-making (about 6). Three note uneven shared-deck quality (HSK Lord, Notelyn, VerbPal).
- **Quizlet:** praised for its library and easy start. Criticized for paywalls (about 8) and weak spacing (about 7).
- **Brainscape:** praised for its clean interface and confidence rating. Criticized for price or a limited free tier (about 7).
- **RemNote:** praised for joining notes and cards. Criticized for complexity (about 7, including its own blog).
- **Mochi:** praised for minimalism and Markdown. Criticized for its small community (2) and sync or export limits as volume grows (1).
- **Knowt:** praised as a free Quizlet substitute. Criticized for AI errors and rough edges.

### Who faded, who's new
- **Closed:** Tinycards (2020) [SAID: Wikipedia, AlternativeTo]. [StudyBlue, ended 2020 after Chegg's 2018 purchase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StudyBlue) (snippet). Fresh Memory (Wikipedia). Flashcards+ by Chegg and IDoRecall were in the 2016 and 2022 lists and in none of the 31 [INFERRED].
- **Renamed:** [AnkiApp to AlgoApp](https://www.algoapp.ai/ankiapp-is-now-algoapp.html). [Anki Pro to Noji](https://www.mindomax.com/anki-pro-vs-anki), June 2025 per a single rival source. [Anki's FAQ disowns both](https://docs.ankiweb.net/faqs/anki-knockoffs). StudySmarter to Vaia. Lists still mix old names.
- **Changed:** [SuperMemo dropped legacy Windows and Android clients, Sept 2025; Anki's stewardship passed to AnkiHub, Feb 2026; Quizlet retired Q-Chat, June 2025](https://www.mindomax.com/spaced-repetition-apps-updates-2026) [CLAIMED; a rival's blog, unverified here]. RemNote's May 2026 list still calls SuperMemo Windows-first.
- **Lasting:** Quizlet and Cram appear across 2016, 2022 and 2026; Anki and Brainscape since at least 2022.
- **New:** Knowt, absent in 2016 and 2022, is in 11 of 31. AI makers fill the lists' lower half: Retain, Maeve, StudyCards AI, Scholarly, FlashRecall, Gizmo, NotebookLM, Piply. SourceForge's 50 Anki alternatives are mostly AI study tools [INFERRED]. [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/category/education-and-reference/flashcards-learning/) dates Hivemind and Ricotta to 2025, and Linglass, Dumka, GapLearn and Quizfreely to 2026. Small Mac apps are new too (NotchWise, FlashCard, Revu), on none of the lists; another, Avorio, is on Mindomax's.

## Evidence against
- **"Anki leads":** six of its 29 mentions are only as the thing to replace. Lifehacker, the one mainstream list, omits it. Toolradar, which scores by review volume, puts it in its 11–22 band with Quizlet first (a search snippet of its sister page said Duolingo first, so that rank moves). Its count is also inflated: "Anki alternatives" was one of my search terms.
- **"The lists are ads":** rivals don't always flatter themselves. Eight place Anki or Quizlet above themselves, Scholarly lists its own faults, and Mindomax discloses the conflict. Anki's strengths are conceded even where it would pay not to, which makes those mentions more credible [INFERRED].
- **"Quizlet is hated":** Trustpilot's 1.4 sits beside 4.5 on G2/Capterra and 4.3 on Play. Trustpilot draws complaints; 88 reviews a year against hundreds of thousands of ratings is a small, self-chosen group.
- **Brainscape's 4.7 may not compare:** 190 reviews a year, about twice Quizlet's, from a far smaller app, suggests it asks for reviews, though the page didn't say so [INFERRED].
- **"Nobody covers widgets":** I searched in English, and search engines rank these sites' SEO pages highly, so a small enthusiast list could be missed. Other angles (06, 20, 25) may find real reception.
