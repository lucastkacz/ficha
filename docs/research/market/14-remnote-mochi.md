# 14 remnote-mochi
Sources read: 60, in full or nearly, 7 of them single Hacker News comments (plus about 15 seen only as search results, marked as such). Covered from 2020 to 2026.

Read on 2026-11-05. Review tallies were made by the reading tool from Apple's feeds, so treat them as rough. First sentences are given whole because the angle asks for them; RemNote's App Store one was cut off by the reader.

## Findings

### Sheet: RemNote
- **Who it's for:** students, medicine above all. `[CLAIMED]` "Trusted by 1,000,000+ students" ([site](https://www.remnote.com/)); it sells MCAT plans in its [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/remnote-notes-flashcards/id1545429784). `[INFERRED]` At least 6 of the latest 50 US reviews name school or professional exams. Third-party profiles seen in search disagree: one says a million, another "hundreds of thousands" ([Yespress](https://yespress.io/remnote) among them).
- **Main features:** block notes where a marker turns a line into a card; AI cards from notes, PDFs and lectures; PDF annotation; exam scheduler; handwriting. `[CLAIMED]` Writing cards inside notes is about twice as fast ([site](https://www.remnote.com/)).
- **Advantages said:** notes and cards in one place, about 8 of 34 five-star reviews in the [latest 50](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/id=1545429784/sortby=mostrecent/json) (Sep 2025 to Nov 2026). Easier than Anki: 2 of them. `[SAID]`
- **Complaints, counted in two feed pages, 100 reviews:**
  - [Newest 50](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/id=1545429784/sortby=mostrecent/json): 10 are one or two stars. Paywall or price 6, AI features or credits 5, bugs 5, confusing UI 4. Sync complaints 0, and sync is praised.
  - [Older 50](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=2/id=1545429784/sortby=mostrecent/json) (Sep 2023 to Sep 2025): 5 are one or two stars. Bugs 13, mobile 13 (freezing on open, indenting by accident while scrolling, leaving the app ejects the edit), UI 4, paywall 0.
  - `[INFERRED]` Complaints moved from the mobile app's bugs to price and AI. Two reviews expected flashcards to be free, and one objects to handwriting moving behind the paywall (Oct 2026).
  - [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/remnote.com): 3.0 from 12 reviews, uninvited, 8 shown; 2 are about billing or paywall, one feeling baited by features moved behind it (2023 `[OLD]`).
  - A [May 2025 Hacker News comment](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44030738): left for Mochi after months, blocks "clunky and complicated", AI overrated. An [April 2026 review](https://tldv.io/ru/blog/remnote/) (publisher sells a meeting tool): failed PDF upload, wrong language detection, Pro+AI dear for students.
  - Rival's view, so weigh lightly: importing Anki drops custom CSS, template JavaScript and on-the-fly speech, and FSRS is an opt-in beta ([Mindomax](https://www.mindomax.com/remnote-vs-anki), sells its own app, 2026).
- **Price `[CLAIMED]`:** Free has unlimited notes and cards, but 3 PDFs, 5 image occlusions and 250 AI credits a month. Pro is $8 a month ($96 billed yearly), Pro with AI $18 ($216 yearly) ([pricing](https://www.remnote.com/pricing)). Paid monthly it's $10 and $20 ([Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/note-taking/remnote/), Oct 2026, third party; the [help page](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6084981-remnote-pro-subscriptions), Aug 2026, gives $20). The App Store charges more: $14.99 a month or $149.99 a year, $29.99 or $299.99 with AI, up to $399.99 with MCAT ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/remnote-notes-flashcards/id1545429784)). Students get 25% off, poorer regions get discounts, refunds run 30 days.
- **How it changed:** in January 2023 trials became usage limits, and old subscribers kept their price ([help](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6891970-new-pricing-plans-2023) `[OLD]`). A lifetime plan exists, with no price shown. Costbench says its server features last 5 years (one source). One review calls its price too high (Oct 2026). A 2021 forum post, seen in search only, gives $300 `[OLD]`.
- **Why people come and leave:** they come for one tool instead of two, for AI cards, and from Quizlet. They leave over a clunky UI, mobile bugs and limits moved behind the paywall. I found no evidence of a wave back to Anki.
- **Small-moment features:** no widget, lock-screen view, menu-bar item or watch app found. The listing mentions to-do "reminders" only. One review praises email review reminders (Feb 2026). A widget search found nothing either way.
- **First sentences:** site: "The AI note taking tool that actually helps you learn." App Store subtitle: "Replace: Notion, Quizlet, Anki". App Store: "RemNote is an all-in-one learning tool that combines note-taking, knowledge management, flashcards, and spaced repetition…"
- **Alive:** yes. iOS 1.29.7, released a day before reading; 4.8 from 1.7K US ratings. Seed round in 2021 from General Catalyst, about $2.8M to $3.5M, sources differ, search results only ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/readwise-vs-remnote)). Revenue unknown.

### Sheet: Mochi
- **Who it's for:** people who want Anki with a nicer face, and language learners, Korean and Japanese above all to judge by its [changelog](https://mochi.cards/changelog.html) and [Bunpro thread](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/if-you-dont-like-anki-consider-giving-mochi-a-try/59955) (2023 `[OLD]`). `[INFERRED]`
- **Main features `[CLAIMED]`:** Markdown notes and cards, linked notes, local-first storage, dictionaries, speech, image search, auto-translation, AI fields, an API ([site](https://mochi.cards/)).
- **Advantages said:** interface and simplicity. About 12 of 22 non-maker Hacker News comments call it nicer or better designed than Anki, one lukewarm ("okay"), none hostile ([search](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=mochi%20cards%20anki&tags=comment&hitsPerPage=30), 2021 to 2026; 8 more are its maker's own posts). About 6 of 49 App Store reviews say "Anki alternative" or better than Anki. It can also act as a notebook, since cards enter review only when chosen ([comment, 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47738493)). Independent reviews: [Borretti, 2022](https://borretti.me/article/first-impressions-mochi) `[OLD]`, [a blogger who left Anki, 2022](https://slims-hhofner-com.vercel.app/blog/on-quitting-anki) `[OLD]`.
- **Complaints, [49 US reviews](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/id=1507775056/sortby=mostrecent/json), 2020 to 2026:** average about 4.0 in the feed against 4.6 on the listing. Bugs 8, confusing UI 8, feature gaps 10 (sorting, studying one deck, answer buttons), corrupted or lost cards 2, large storage 2, price 1, accessibility 1 ("button" labels for VoiceOver). Others say: no note types and no full backup ([Borretti](https://borretti.me/article/first-impressions-mochi) `[OLD]`); no Yomichan integration, web-based feel on mobile, too invested in Anki (Bunpro, 19 replies `[OLD]`). Its changelog admits a macOS Tahoe data-loss fix (April 2026).
- **Price `[CLAIMED]`:** Free offline forever. Pro is $5 a month (billed yearly it saves $12) with sync, 10 GB and AI limits ([pricing](https://mochi.cards/pricing)). Already $5 in 2022 (Borretti). Paying happens on the website, hard to work out for one [reviewer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mochi-cards/id1507775056) (2025). Users regretted having no lifetime option (Bunpro `[OLD]`). One says "a subscriber just because of the cloud sync" ([2024](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39168518)).
- **Why people come and leave:** they come from Anki's interface. They stay away over Anki's ecosystem (Bunpro `[OLD]`).
- **Small-moment features:** daily reminder push notifications since April 2025 ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mochi-cards/id1507775056)). A [forum request](https://forum.mochi.cards/posts/358/improvement-to-the-notification-feature), about 7 months old, calls them "simple and pretty basic" and wants them tied to due cards. One review asked for a daily reminder in 2024, before they shipped. No widget or watch app mentioned. A badge request exists, seen in search only.
- **First sentences:** site headline: "Spaced repetition flashcards made easy". Site and App Store: "Take notes and make flashcards using markdown, then study them using spaced repetition." Subtitle: "Simple, intelligent flashcards".
- **Alive:** yes, frequent releases; [26.10.1 on 2026-10-23](https://mochi.cards/changelog.html), one developer named on the listing. 4.6 from 175 US ratings. [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/1507775056) estimates 27.5K installs and 658 monthly users, weak and search-result only. Its [forum](https://forum.mochi.cards) is active but small, with no widget topics visible.

### Sheet: Obsidian's spaced repetition plugins
- **Who it's for:** people already keeping notes in Obsidian. `[INFERRED]`
- **Spaced Repetition plugin:** 627,576 downloads, 2,609 stars, 1.15.4 about 5 months ago ([ObsidianStats](https://obsidianstats.com/plugins/obsidian-spaced-repetition)); 325 issues, FSRS and SM-2 ([GitHub](https://github.com/st3v3nmw/obsidian-spaced-repetition)). First sentence: "Fight the forgetting curve by reviewing flashcards & notes using the FSRS or the SM-2 spaced repetition algorithms." Free.
- **Others:** a [hub list](https://publish.obsidian.md/hub/02+-+Community+Expansions/02.01+Plugins+by+Category/Spaced+Repetition+Plugins) of 9 has 4 Anki bridges and a Mochi exporter; Obsidian_to_Anki is listed as no longer developed. [Anki Integration](https://obsidianstats.com/plugins/anki-integration): 4,455 downloads. [Klomimory](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/klomimory-free-srs-flashcards-plugin-for-language-learning/118242), new in Sep 2026, got no replies.
- **Complaints:** one who tried it found it "not even remotely close" to their needs ([blog, 2026](https://lesleylai.info/en/flashcards/)). 2021 thread: bugs and button confusion ([forum](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/plugin-for-flashcards-note-level-spaced-repetition-all-inside-obsidian/16498?page=3) `[OLD]`).
- **Obsidian's price `[CLAIMED]`:** free without limits; Sync $4 to $5 a month; Publish $8 to $10; Commercial $50 a year, optional; Catalyst $25 once ([pricing](https://obsidian.md/pricing)). Revenue of about $2M is a third-party estimate (Latka, Fueler; search results, weak).
- **Small-moment features:** [Popup Review](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/sr-popup-review) shows cards in "a small always-on-top popup that appears at regular intervals". Desktop only, skipping full-screen apps only on Windows, 205 downloads, updated about 3 weeks ago. Note Review Reminder offers OS notifications (search result). No widget found.
- **First sentences:** Obsidian site: "Sharpen your thinking." then "The free and flexible app for your private thoughts." App Store: "Obsidian is a powerful knowledge base that works on top of a local folder of plain text Markdown files." 4.5 from 2.8K US ratings ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/obsidian-connected-notes/id1557175442)), version 1.15.0, 3 days before reading.

### Sheet: Logseq
- **Who it's for:** outliners who want local, open-source notes. Flashcards are built in.
- **Complaints:** the team said in 2023 it had no capacity for flashcards, and in October 2024 that FSRS would come with the DB version ([thread](https://discuss.logseq.com/t/fsrs-for-flashcard/13824)). A reported bug in the old algorithm, basic features, no statistics ([post, 2023](https://discuss.logseq.com/t/how-was-your-experience-moving-from-anki-to-logseq-for-flashcards-and-srs/19458) `[OLD]`). DB version: "still no custom SRS settings" ([Jan 2025](https://discuss.logseq.com/t/2-way-anki-logseq-flashcards-sync/30748), 1 post). Community sync to Anki goes one way only.
- **Price:** free; Sync reported as a beta for $5 to $15 a month backers, sources conflict, none official (search results).
- **Small-moment features:** none found.
- **First sentences:** site title: "Logseq: A privacy-first, open-source knowledge base". App Store: "A local, open-source knowledge base Logseq is a joyful, open-source outliner that works on top of local plain-text…"
- **Alive:** yes, slowly on stores. [Desktop 2.0.2 beta](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/releases), October 2026, lists "Improved flashcard practice"; a nightly on 25 October. The [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/logseq/id1601013908?l=en-GB) still shows 0.10.9 from April 2024, 4.4 from 111 ratings (German store). The DB iOS beta is under review.

### Do people want flashcards inside their notes, or apart?
- **Sample:** the reading tool sorted [31 Hacker News comments](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=obsidian%20flashcards&tags=comment&hitsPerPage=40) on Obsidian and flashcards, 2022 to 2026, from which I re-sorted its one-line summaries and read 2 in full (one had been sorted wrongly). About 8 review inside notes; about 9 write in notes and review in Anki or another app; about 3 keep them apart without linking; about 3 are plugin builders promoting; the rest are unclear. `[INFERRED]` The search term selects note-takers, so this overstates "inside" among learners generally.
- **Inside `[SAID]`:** the appeal is no duplicate work and no second app: "I don't need to maintain two separate apps" ([2024](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=42515489)); "so sick" ([RemNote, 2025](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44024268)). Matuschak argues that using two tools means doubling the work, and that prompts in prose have context ([note](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/The_mnemonic_medium_can_be_extended_to_one’s_personal_notes), undated). 3 of 8 Logseq comments stay in Logseq for this ([search](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=logseq%20flashcards&tags=comment&hitsPerPage=30), 2021 to 2025).
- **Bridge `[SAID]`:** this is the most common real workflow among heavy users. They write and link in notes, review in Anki for its engine, and link back. The 3 posters who describe a setup in an [April 2026 Obsidian thread](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/myriad-anki-plugins-seeking-advice/113776) do this; the starter had left the plugin for Anki. [May 2025](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44021805): cards at the bottom of each note, synced to Anki. A [2021 thread](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/why-use-obsidian-and-anki-together/11661) `[OLD]`, 3 posts, reads the same. The [Beeminder thread](https://forum.beeminder.com/t/spaced-repetition-obsidian-plugin/10550) (2022 `[OLD]`): 2 of 3 favor Anki, "I would rather collect all spaced repetition in Anki"; the third refuses a second app.
- **Apart, and split by content `[SAID]`:** one Chinese reviewer keeps connected knowledge in RemNote and word cards with audio and video in Anki, each doing its job ([sspai, 2021](https://sspai.com/post/65848) `[OLD]`, 1 person). Another says Anki can replace notes, because notes go unreviewed ([2023](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=38671208) `[OLD]`). One Logseq user fears flashcards bloat a notes app ([2025](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=42594060)).
- `[INFERRED]` Inside-notes wins among those whose main act is taking notes: students with lectures. Separate review wins among those with many cards or heavy media, and those who value Anki's scheduler. Almost no one in these sources asks for cards with no notes behind them, but this angle's sources would not find such people; other angles should.

### Who pays?
- **Students pay for AI, PDFs and exam tools, not for cards.** RemNote's cards and notes are free, and a reviewer notes that paid plans are about AI, scans and uploads (Sep 2026). One [paying user](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43910780) praises AI. One review says the plan's credits burn quickly (Sep 2025). `[SAID]` 6 of 50 recent reviews object to the charges.
- **Hobbyists and language learners pay small sums for sync.** Mochi at $5, and Obsidian Sync at $4 to $5. Support for the maker comes up too ([2024](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39168518)). `[INFERRED]` Sync across devices is the recurring paid hook in both.
- **Plugins earn nothing:** those above are free. `[INFERRED]`
- **No public revenue or paying-user numbers** for RemNote, Mochi or Logseq.

## Evidence against
- **"Notes and cards together wins":** 627K plugin downloads count installs, not use. Most heavy users in forums still review in Anki. Praise for RemNote clusters in one [May 2025 thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44020591), where its founder was present; of [40 RemNote comments](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=remnote&tags=comment&hitsPerPage=40), 23 positive, 3 negative, 3 from the founder.
- **"Students won't pay":** RemNote reviews praise paid plans too (exam scheduler, AI tutor).
- **"Mochi's bugs are the story":** its feed spans six years and many reviews are feature requests; recent reviews are mixed but not collapsing. The 4.6 listing rating and the 4.0 feed average differ because written reviews skew harsher. `[INFERRED]`
- **"Price is a RemNote problem":** older reviews showed none, so the shift is recent; it may also reflect more free users seeing AI limits, not a harsher paywall. I could not confirm any 2026 paywall change except that one review.
- **"People want small-moment review in these apps":** requests are few: 1 Mochi forum post, 1 review, one 205-download plugin. Silence may reflect who these tools attract. `[INFERRED]`
