# 16 memrise-language-apps
Sources read: about 70 pages in full or nearly, including 11 Apple review feed pages and 3 Hacker News searches (plus about 25 seen only as search results, marked as such). Covered from 2017 to 2026.

Read on 2026-11-05. Review tallies were made by the reading tool from Apple's US feeds, so treat them as rough. First sentences are given whole because the angle asks for them. Store prices are US; store dates without a year are taken as 2026. The WaniKani forum is mostly people who already pay, as its own members say.

## Findings

### What users felt when Memrise left community courses
- **Timeline.** `[CLAIMED]` Courses left the app and main site on 31 March 2024 for a separate site; access was extended in November 2024; "We are not shutting Community Courses down" in November 2025 ([Memrise blog](https://www.memrise.com/blog/changes-to-the-memrise-app)). In 2026 about 800 came back as word lists, and the old site stays as an archive ([Memrise](https://explore.memrise.com/community-courses)). `[SAID, one source]` A 2019 split was reversed in February 2020 after criticism; a "sunset" was announced in November 2023 and the forum closed that December ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memrise)).
- **Memrise admits the weight.** `[CLAIMED]` "For many of you, they're the main reason you use Memrise." ([blog](https://www.memrise.com/blog/changes-to-the-memrise-app))
- **Frustration, and a stuck feeling.** `[SAID]` A long-time user and blogger called it the end of an era and moved to LingQ, not Anki ([May 2024](https://eurolinguiste.com/memrise-is-doing-away-with-user-generated-content-now-what/), affiliate links). In 21 posts on an Anki add-on thread, ex-users are worn out by repeated changes, and course authors feel leaving costs their work ([Anki forum, 2023](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/an-alternative-to-memrise2anki-support-thread/30084) `[OLD]`). Of 22 Hacker News comments on Memrise since 2024, 10 say it got worse, 6 mention lost user content, 3 point to Anki, 3 come from people building their own replacement, 6 praise it ([search](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=memrise&tags=comment&hitsPerPage=40&numericFilters=created_at_i%3E1704067200)).
- **What exactly was lost.** `[SAID]` The phone: no app and no offline ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memrise)). Rare languages: Lojban and Cornish threads, second-hand, seen only in search results; no official Finnish ([Hacker News, 2024](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=memrise&tags=comment&hitsPerPage=40&numericFilters=created_at_i%3E1704067200)). User-made memory aids, in 3 of those comments.
- **Moving means losing progress.** `[SAID]` One user moving about 4,000 words wanted their due dates carried into Anki ([Anki forum, Feb 2024](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/importing-progress-from-memrise-and-some-other-questions/40962.md)). `[CLAIMED]` Polidict's importer drops progress, audio and images ([Polidict](https://polidict.com/en/blog/memrise-import), sells an app). A [community tool](https://github.com/realmayus/memrise2anki-extension) converts courses to Anki decks; a later fork was updated in June 2025 (search only).
- **It fades.** `[INFERRED]` Reviews naming removed courses, per 50 on the [feed](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=10/id=635966718/sortby=mostrecent/json): 5 (Dec 2024 to Feb 2025), 4 ([Apr to Jul 2025](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=8/id=635966718/sortby=mostrecent/json)), about 4 ([Aug to Oct 2025](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=6/id=635966718/sortby=mostrecent/json)), 1 ([May to Jul 2026](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=2/id=635966718/sortby=mostrecent/json)), 0 in [the latest 50](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/id=635966718/sortby=mostrecent/json). The feed stops at December 2024, so the peak isn't visible. In 2026 AI, bugs and billing took over.

### Sheet: Memrise
- **For:** `[CLAIMED]` people with "a specific reason to learn"; beginners to intermediates. 87M registered ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memrise)).
- **Features `[CLAIMED]`:** native-speaker videos, recording yourself, AI chat, Exam Prep, Stories, AI-made Mems (May 2026) ([site](https://www.memrise.com/), [blog](https://www.memrise.com/blog/changes-to-the-memrise-app)). Praised: about 12 of latest 50 say it works, about 6 like real people.
- **Complaints:** latest 50, average 3.8: bugs about 9, price about 6, content gone about 3, UI about 3, AI 2. May to July 2026, 50 reviews: AI forced into lessons is the top theme, plus endless loading. [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/memrise.com): 1.6 from 143; of 20 latest, 11 no support reply, 6 bugs, 5 charges after cancelling, 2 AI, 2 courses.
- **Price `[CLAIMED]`:** $24.99 a month, $61.99 a year, $329.99 lifetime, offer at $131.99 ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/memrise-speak-a-new-language/id635966718)). A [review](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/memrise-review-good-beginners-dont-overuse/) gives $39.99 monthly (Nov 2025, affiliate links). Regional spread $9 to $25 (aggregator, search only). I found no record of an increase.
- **Moves:** arrive for real speakers and for something beyond Duolingo (about 8 reviews compare them, both ways). Leave over lost courses, AI, billing.
- **Small moments:** no widget found. `[SAID, search only]` A third-party [Chrome extension](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/ruzu-memrise-pop-ups/eoepijkdcdnafobigopfohlobdhnegkm) popped up Memrise questions; its listing wouldn't load, so usage is unknown.
- **First sentences:** site: "Learn a language. Memrise is authentic, useful & personalised." Store: "Memrise is a language learning app for people who have a specific reason to learn."
- **Alive:** v2026.10.21, 23 Oct; 4.8 from 218K.

### What brought people to pay for WaniKani
Eleven forum threads and one Hacker News search, 2017 to 2026. `[SAID]`, biased toward payers.
1. **Someone else decides.** The strongest reason. Fixed order, chosen vocabulary, ready mnemonics: about 11 of 20 posts favor it over Anki, on structure above all ([2022](https://community.wanikani.com/t/wanikani-vs-anki/57819) `[OLD]`). Same in [2025](https://community.wanikani.com/t/worth-subscribing/71999?page=4) and [2022](https://community.wanikani.com/t/is-premium-worth-buying%EF%BC%9F/55523?page=2) `[OLD]`, where 5 of 12 say yes, 0 no, 2 mixed.
2. **It stops overload.** Lessons are gated ([2025](https://community.wanikani.com/t/worth-subscribing/71999?page=4)).
3. **It can't be cheated.** Typing replaces Anki's self-rating ([2022](https://community.wanikani.com/t/wanikani-vs-anki/57819) `[OLD]`; 1 Hacker News comment, 2025).
4. **Free levels first, then a December sale.** Advised in nearly every price thread: try, buy a year, then lifetime on sale ([2026](https://community.wanikani.com/t/lifetime-membership-is-it-worth-it/73200), [2017](https://community.wanikani.com/t/worth-the-money/19425) `[OLD]`).
5. **Lifetime suits people who stop and return.** About 13 people, January 2026: slow learners value no renewal decision, and one credits it for not quitting during illness ([thread](https://community.wanikani.com/t/lifetime-membership-is-it-worth-it/73200)). In [2022](https://community.wanikani.com/t/how-worth-while-is-the-lifetime-subscription/59863) `[OLD]`, 6 yes, 0 no.
6. **Paying as commitment,** and the community: a few each ([2020](https://community.wanikani.com/t/annual-membership-or-lifetime/48105), [2017](https://community.wanikani.com/t/is-wanikani-worth-the-price/18192) `[OLD]`).

[Stale product](https://community.wanikani.com/t/wanikanis-lack-of-improvement/63933?page=6), 2023 to 2025. [Hacker News](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=wanikani&tags=comment&hitsPerPage=40): 12 praise, 6 complain, 3 say use Anki.
- **Price `[CLAIMED]`:** $9, $89, $299 lifetime ([help](https://knowledge.wanikani.com/account-and-membership/payment-and-billing/subscription-plans/)). `[SAID]` About $200 in the winter sale, from 12 December 2025. First levels free, 3 or 4 ([review](https://www.mezzoguild.com/wanikani-review), inconsistent). No increase found.
- **Moves:** leave for Anki, Renshuu, jpdb, or when money ends. Some move progress into Anki.
- **Small moments:** no official iOS app; a lifetime member still [asked in April 2026](https://community.wanikani.com/t/wanikani-ios-app-please/74215), unanswered by staff. - **Alive:** yes; forum active, © 2026. No changelog read.

## Evidence against
- **Memrise's anger was loud but maybe small.** Its store rating stays 4.8, 33 of the latest 50 give four or five stars, and only 13 or so of 150 reviews in 2024-25 name courses. Trustpilot's top pains are support and billing. Course quality also varied, so the loss wasn't pure ([Clozemaster forum, 2023](https://forum.clozemaster.com/t/my-thoughts-after-one-year-with-clozemaster-german/40602) `[OLD]`).
- **WaniKani payers aren't all content.** A rival forum leans to Anki, one user calls it [overpriced and rigid](https://community.bunpro.jp/t/anki-vs-wanikani/83766), another [only stays because lifetime is prepaid](https://community.wanikani.com/t/wanikanis-lack-of-improvement/63933?page=6). A poster warns [the people who keep paying](https://community.wanikani.com/t/is-wanikani-worth-the-price/18192) naturally recommend it.
- **Duolingo's backlashes barely show.** The AI-first storm [didn't hurt its numbers](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/07/the-backlash-against-duolingo-going-ai-first-didnt-even-matter/), though growth hit the low end of guidance. A [hearts petition](https://www.change.org/p/reinstate-the-old-heart-system-on-duolingo) has 33 signatures. Daily users grew 29% anyway.
- **Widgets may matter though nobody writes about them.** Duolingo's blog says they do, and reviews rarely name quiet features.
