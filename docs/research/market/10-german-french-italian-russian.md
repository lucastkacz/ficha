# 10 german-french-italian-russian
Sources read: 42 (13 App Store review feeds, about 620 reviews; 29 pages). Covered from 2010 to 2026.

How to read the counts: the feeds are the first page only, read 2026-11-05, newest first, through WebFetch's summary, and I tallied them by hand, so counts are rough and one review can count twice. Review sets are self-selected and lean towards people with a grievance. "Search summary" means I saw it only through WebSearch's digest, not the page.

## Findings

### Across all four languages

- `[SAID]` **Anki's iOS price against free Android and desktop is the most repeated complaint in German and French.** About 18 of 50 in [Anki de] (Dec 2025 to Nov 2026), about 14 of 50 in [Anki fr] (Dec 2022 to Oct 2026), about 8 of 44 in [Anki it] (2010 to 2026), about 5 of 50 in [Anki ru] (2020 to 2026). The same grievance usually comes with "the phone app does less than the desktop": about 12 of 50 in de, about 6 in fr.
- `[SAID]` **The interface is the second complaint.** About 11 of 50 in de, about 6 in fr. On Habr too: [2023 article, `[OLD]`][h759], [2025 comment][h972], [translated 2025 piece][h915].
- `[SAID]` **Quizlet's paywall draws complaints in all four.** Stores' averages on this page: de 3.5 ([8 on price, 4 on paywall of 50][q de]), fr 3.5 ([about 16 of 50][q fr]), it 3.1 ([about 8 of 50, 3 on ads][q it]), ru 2.9 ([about 6 on paywall][q ru]). A Belgian paper notes the same in [2024][libre].
- `[SAID]` **Widgets are asked for, unprompted, in Anki's reviews:** 3 in fr, 1 in it, 1 in ru, 0 in de. Notifications appear less: 2 in ru say they're broken, 1 in de on badges, 1 in fr wants reminders. 5 requests in about 194 reviews is small.
- `[INFERRED]` **Volume of written reviews differs a lot.** A first page of 50 spans 11 months in de, about 4 years in fr, about 6.5 years in ru, and the it store's 44 span 16 years. It suggests far more German-language iOS Anki buyers writing reviews than Italian ones, nothing more exact.

### German

**What they use.**
- `[CLAIMED]` Medicine runs on Anki plus AMBOSS. Ankizin, a non-profit deck of the German medical students' association, reports [at least 20,000 users, about 50,000 cards, 2,784 AnkiHub subscribers, 100+ active students in 18 local groups][gma] (abstract, Sept 2026). Its [site][ankizin] offers it with Discord, WhatsApp and local groups, and also lists Ankiphil for the Physikum. AMBOSS gives away an [Anki add-on][amboss] that needs an account after 50 definitions; a physician's guide says [about 90% of faculties have free campus licences][medir] (the site earns from affiliate codes; AMBOSS's own page says only "most").
- `[SAID]` Medi-Learn threads ([search summary][ml], about 6 threads, undated there but one is from 2019, so `[OLD]`): mostly favourable, several say to write your own cards, one found 7,000 cards too many, one says Anki is a small part of their study.
- `[CLAIMED]` Law: Repetico is pitched [at law students, with publishers' card sets for sale][talent] (undated), and a publisher's app, [Alpmann eCards][alpmann] (store page, search summary), syncs with it.
- `[CLAIMED]` School and vocabulary: phase6, sold to families and libraries, [4.2 of 5 over about 20,000 reviews on a portal; parents complain about family plans and notice periods][p6] (search summary only; the portal lists vouchers). General-purpose: [GoConqr, karteikarte.com, vokabel.org][heise] (2022, `[OLD]`), Vaia.

**What they say of the local apps.**
- `[SAID]` [Repetico de]: average 3.0, 19 of 50 one-star (2022 to 2026). About 10 on bugs and crashes, about 10 on a redesign they dislike, about 7 on price, some asking for a one-time purchase, one saying bought cards stopped working outside the subscription. Developer support gets about 4 good, 3 bad. `[CLAIMED]` It offers [Leitner, SM-2 or FSRS][mindo] (search summary; that page sells an AI tool).
- `[SAID]` [Brainyoo de]: average 3.9 (2024 to 2026). Praised for intuitive use and sync across devices (about 7 each), 5 on login or registration failures, 3 on price, about 6 on a dated interface. A reader of a [2015 article, `[OLD]`][appg] (search summary) left Brainyoo because its export only re-imports into Brainyoo.
- `[SAID]` [Vaia de]: average 4.1 (one month only, Oct to Nov 2026). 5 on price, 4 on paywall, 6 on bugs, AI 3 for and 2 against, 3 on unanswered support; 4 praise the free tier.
- `[CLAIMED]` A vendor's [comparison, `[OLD]` 2023][ssvsanki] pitches StudySmarter as more intuitive and calls out Anki's setup time and sync. It sells a rival.

**Privacy.**
- `[SAID]` **Consumers barely raise it.** Across 250 German-store reviews of Anki, Repetico, Brainyoo, Vaia and Quizlet: 0 direct privacy complaints. One more, in a smaller feed of [21 reviews for a Lernkarten app][dl], says it's unclear where data is stored. One Anki reviewer goes the other way, [missing account-based sync][Anki de].
- `[SAID]` **It shows up on the institution's side.** A person wanting to use AnkiWeb with students [asked about GDPR in Sept 2025; 1 reply, no answer][gdpr]. Anki's FAQ, by search summary, [says it can't sign data-processing agreements with schools][ankifaq]. An EU language-teaching centre's catalogue, also by search summary, [rates Anki's privacy high but notes GDPR isn't mentioned][ecml].
- `[INFERRED]` I found no German article or forum post treating flashcard apps' privacy as a reason to choose one. The searches were thin: see Blockers.

### French

**What they use.**
- `[CLAIMED]` PASS/LAS (first-year medicine): a prep school calls Anki [the reference for memorising][dipl] (May 2026), alongside Koro AI, Notion and Forest. It sells prep and its own card app, and it gives Anki's iOS price as 2.99 €, which [other pages contradict][anki fr page] at 29.99 €.
- `[SAID]` **The "méthode des J" is the local name for spacing by hand.** Students review a chapter at J0, J1, J3, J7, J14, J30, tracked in a spreadsheet. [L'Étudiant's Aug 2026 piece][letud] follows 3 students, none using Anki, and 1 dropped the method as hard to sustain. A tutors' document (search summary, [Rennes, 2022, `[OLD]`][tut]) has one tutor preferring Anki because the J give every notion the same frequency.
- `[CLAIMED]` **Paper and paid decks are a real market here.** [Les Passcards][pass] and [EDN Flash][edn], printed sets of about 200 cards. EDN students [sell ECOS decks on Payhip][payhip], citing their exam rank (seller claims, search summary).
- `[CLAIMED]` Prépa (business-school track): [Major Prépa][major] calls Anki "l'App utilisée par les meilleurs" (Sept 2024, ad-supported media).
- `[CLAIMED]` French-speaking apps: [Wooflash, Deepmemory, Knowt, Vaia][libre] as Quizlet's replacements (2024). Anki isn't in that list.

**What they say.**
- `[CLAIMED]` **Shared decks don't match each faculty's course notes** (polycopiés): [2 pages from the same prep school][dipl2] say so, and it sells its own decks, so its interest is plain. `[INFERRED]` I saw no equivalent German complaint, since Ankizin and Ankiphil are built on one national source (AMBOSS and the 100-day plan).
- `[SAID]` [Anki fr]: 3 medical students (1 lost all decks, 1 credits it with saving their studies, 1 asks for subdecks); 3 on bad translations of the interface.

### Italian

Thinnest of the four, and the evidence is nearly all store reviews.
- `[SAID]` [Anki it], 44 reviews over 16 years: 5 ask for Italian or complain of its translation, all `[OLD]` (2018 to 2023); 2 ask for AI built in (2025, 2026) and 1 can't import the AI-made files on mobile; 1 moved to Mochi because the iPhone app lags (Mar 2026). 0 mention medicine.
- `[SAID]` [Quizlet it]: 3 ask when AI arrives, about 8 object to paying, 1 claims without evidence that it steals data.
- `[SAID]` One Italian on the Anki forum says Anki is [little known in Italy][italy] (search summary, single post).
- `[CLAIMED]` A [University of Padua library guide][padova] (2025, search summary) teaches Anki and warns to check who made a shared deck. An Italian-course seller [rates Anki for vocabulary only][think] (undated).
- `[INFERRED]` **On the medicine test (TOLC-MED, semestre filtro), I found no evidence of Anki use.** What surfaced was [questions traded on Telegram][fanpage] in a 2023 scandal (`[OLD]`, search summary). This is a failure to find, not proof of absence.

### Russian

**What they use.**
- `[SAID]` Mostly languages. [Anki ru] is largely vocabulary, with a few from medicine (2), aviation (1), and exam prep. On Habr, Anki comes with programmers' tooling: TTS add-ons, spreadsheets and [automated deck building][h791] (Feb 2024, `[OLD]`, about 36 comments).
- `[SAID]` **ReWord is the local favourite for English words.** [Average 4.5 over 50 reviews (Jul to Nov 2026)][reword], and its top praise is a low one-time price, about 11. Also named: [EnLearner][h963] and [Skyeng, Momo English][h984] in Habr comments; both threads sit under an app maker's own articles.
- `[SAID]` Long-term users exist: daily use ["как чистка зубов"][h465] (2019, `[OLD]`), [6 and 7+ years][Anki ru] in reviews. A reader in that 2019 thread asks for a macOS app that sends reminders.

**What they say.**
- `[SAID]` **Media failing on iOS is unusually common in this store:** about 6 of 50 on images, audio or video not showing or playing.
- `[SAID]` **Flashcards themselves are doubted on Habr.** Commenters say it's [for memorising what you already learned][h322] (2017, `[OLD]`), [suits only literal facts][h465] (2019, `[OLD]`), or [a paper notebook does as well][h963] (Nov 2025). About 2 to 3 per thread.
- `[CLAIMED]` A Russian app's founder [objects to the four buttons][h1046] as shifting the algorithm's work onto the user; another commenter [advises against Easy and Hard][h791]. The first sells a rival.
- `[CLAIMED]` A 2025 translated piece presents [FSRS as a marked relief over SM-2][h915] (47 comments unread), and FSRS is known well enough here to reach [Obsidian plugin articles][hobs] (search summary).

**Paying from Russia.**
- `[CLAIMED]` Guides say [Russian cards don't work for AnkiMobile or AnkiHub, leaving a foreign card or an intermediary][dtf1] and the same [for Quizlet][dtf2], and that [the old App Store route ended on 1 April 2026, affecting 16 million][rbc] (search summary of a page in RBC's companies section, which I couldn't open). All of them sell or promote a payment service, so treat the size as unverified.
- `[SAID]` 5 of 50 Quizlet ru reviews mention it directly (2026), and 3 mention repeated human-check prompts, 4 to 5 lost access. 0 of 50 Anki ru and 0 of 50 ReWord ru reviews mention payment.

## Evidence against

- **On price:** 5 of 50 German reviewers call Anki worth it, some "every cent", and it stays many people's only serious option: 5-star reviews in every store. A Russian reviewer writes "Стоит каждого цента" (Jan 2025). Italian and Russian complaints are also far fewer.
- **On "privacy doesn't matter to Germans":** reviews aren't where people argue about privacy; I couldn't read Reddit or gutefrage, and a privacy-minded user may never write one. So 0 of 250 shows it isn't a common complaint, not that it isn't a reason to choose.
- **On Anki as the German medical standard:** the numbers are Ankizin's and AMBOSS's own, and the guide earns from affiliates. Medi-Learn posters say making cards costs too much time and that Anki is a small part of their study. [Published studies][pmc] that surfaced (search summary) are American and mixed outside Step 1.
- **On Anki in France:** L'Étudiant's piece and La Libre's five apps don't mention it. So "the reference" comes from a seller and an ad-supported site.
- **On AI wanted in Italy:** German and French Quizlet reviewers are about as often against its AI as for it (de 4 against, 2 for; fr 4 against).
- **On Habr's scepticism:** those threads sit under app makers' articles and attract self-builders; the same site has the warmest long-term users.
- **On the Russian payment wall:** no Anki or ReWord reviewer mentions it, though the ReWord lifetime purchase may predate April 2026.

## Blockers

- **Medi-Learn forums:** every thread tried (5) gives "too many redirects" in WebFetch. Seen only by search summary. Lucas could open e.g. [this thread][ml] and export it.
- **Reddit:** not read, by the brief; r/de, r/Italia and Russian subreddits unseen. Mirrors appeared in results and I left them alone, per the brief.
- **Local forums never surfaced:** gutefrage, jeuxvideo.com, remede.org, Skuola.net, 4pda, Pikabu and vc.ru reviews. WebSearch is US-only and targeted queries returned English SEO pages. Italian suffers most. A native-language browser session would help.
- **PDFs unreadable:** Rennes tutorat's two sheets, Padua's guide, Köln's Anki flyer and Tambov's paper came back compressed, and the brief bars shell tools. Saved under the session's `tool-results` folder as `webfetch-*.pdf` if someone runs `pdftotext`.
- **Refused:** RBC Companies (401); Capterra DE, GetApp DE and G2 IT (403); App Store pages for Flashcard Hero and Cogni, German-store apps worth a look (429). The Repetico pricing URL I tried gave 404.
- **The feeds:** only page 1 each; WebFetch summarises them, so these counts could be off by a few.
