# 18 readwise
Sources read: 42, plus about 76 Hacker News comments read through one index, and search snippets marked as such. Covered from 2018 to 2026.

The stance counts below come from small, self-selected samples. Hacker News (HN) and the Mac Power Users forum (MPU) lean towards paying power users. No source gives retention, subscriber or daily-review completion figures. Reddit was not read.

## Findings

### Readwise (highlights and Daily Review)

- **Who it's for:** `[CLAIMED]` "productivity-oriented power users", said its co-founder, against chasing a mass market ([Ness Labs interview, 2021, `[OLD]`](https://nesslabs.com/readwise-featured-tool)).
- **Main features `[CLAIMED]`:** imports highlights from Kindle, Apple Books, Instapaper and more; Daily Review by email and app, also as audio; spaced repetition; tags, notes; paper-book scanning; streaks; export to Obsidian and Notion; an MCP server ([site](https://readwise.io/), [changelog](https://readwise.io/changelog), both read 2026-11-05).
- **How Daily Review picks `[CLAIMED]`:** each highlight's chance scales with its source's share of the library, and falls after each showing. Users tune sources up or down. "Mastery" cards use recall half-lives of 7, 14 or 28 days ([docs](https://docs.readwise.io/readwise/docs/faqs/reviewing-highlights), read 2026-11-05).
- **Advantages `[SAID]`:** the sync and the resurfacing. Product Hunt: 20 reviews, 4.8; of 13 readable, 3 mention daily review, 0 price, 0 bugs ([reviews](https://www.producthunt.com/products/readwise/reviews), about 5 months to 4 years old).
- **Complaints `[SAID]`, with counts:**
  - Price: about 5 of 76 HN comments, May 2025 to Nov 2026 ([one](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44068545), [two](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44695244), [three](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43225571)). Two MPU posts in Dec 2022 cancelled over cost and moved to the free Bookcision (seen as [search excerpts](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/thrilled-with-obsidian-readwise-zotero-workflow/24997), `[OLD]`).
  - Apps feel clunky: about 4 of 16 posts in [one MPU thread](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/in-praise-of-readwise/40974) (June 2025), about the iPad app or Reader; one more called the iPhone app old-fashioned ([MPU, 2024](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/alternative-to-readwise-for-capture-highlights-in-paper-books/36791), `[OLD]`).
  - The two apps don't fully join: 1 of 13 Product Hunt reviews and [1 HN comment](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44065123) (May 2025).
  - Bugs: the Daily Review count resetting ([store review, snippet](https://apps.apple.com/dk/app/readwise/id1476885528), undated); push stopping after a restored backup ([help, snippet](https://help.readwise.io/article/98-why-did-push-notifications-stop-in-the-ios-readwise-app)).
- **Price and how it changed:**
  - Before Feb 2024: $7.99 a month billed yearly, $8.99 monthly; Lite $4.49.
  - From Feb 18, 2024, new subscribers: $9.99 and $12.99; Lite $5.59. Existing subscribers kept their rate. It was the first change since charging began in 2018 ([Techpresso, 2026-11-02](https://academy.techpresso.co/reviews/is-readwise-reader-worth-it), from archived pages; no affiliate disclosure, says nothing either way).
  - Late Oct 2026: four plans, Full $9.99, Reader only $7.99, Highlights only $7.99, Lite $5.59, all billed yearly ([pricing page](https://readwise.io/pricing), read 2026-11-05). `[INFERRED]` Reader or Highlights alone are new: an archive of Oct 19 showed two plans, per Techpresso.
  - 30-day trial, no free tier; 50% off for students and, by email, some other groups and countries ([Reader page](https://readwise.io/read)). The trial was 60 days in [2021](https://rishikeshs.com/readwise-review/) `[OLD]`.
- **Why people move to it `[SAID]`:** Pocket's shutdown is the biggest visible flow. About 7 of the 76 HN comments are Pocket or Instapaper users naming it as their move ([one](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44600746), [two](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44064500)), mostly about Reader, not review.
- **Why people leave `[SAID]`:** 6 of 76 HN comments say they left. Reasons: not enough use for $10 a month, a self-hosted tool, another reader, emailing links to themselves, [forgetting to open it](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44345279), and [a username-grab notice for Readwise Books](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50320837) (Oct 2026). A rival's blog says many use one or two parts ([ContextBolt, 2026](https://contextbolt.com/blog/readwise-alternatives/)), with no data.
- **Surfaces:**
  - Email: the main one; can be set to "Never" ([docs](https://docs.readwise.io/readwise/docs/faqs/reviewing-highlights)).
  - Push notification: one a day, about an hour after the email time; iOS and Android ([docs](https://docs.readwise.io/readwise/docs/faqs/mobile-apps)).
  - Home Screen widget: medium and large. It shows the first highlight with a prompt until the review is done, then a random highlight every 5 minutes ([changelog, 2021](https://readwise.io/changelog/ios-widget), `[OLD]`; same text in today's docs).
  - Lock screen, menu bar, watch: not found in docs, changelog or the store page. `[INFERRED]` Absent. The store lists no Apple Watch.
  - How received: almost no one writes about the widget or push. I found no praise or complaint about either beyond the bugs above.
- **First sentence:** site, after the title "Get the most out of what you read": "Buy, read, and track all your books in one beautiful place" ([site](https://readwise.io/), now led by the new Books app). App Store: "Readwise helps you get the most out of what you read…", cut here for length ([store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/readwise/id1476885528)).
- **Alive:** yes. Store version 3.5.2, Oct 21; 4.8 from about 1.5K ratings. The Highlights changelog's last entry is July 2, 2025. Readwise Books, an e-book store and tracker, launched in beta around Oct 30, 2026; the company is bootstrapped ([TechCrunch, 2026-11-02](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/readwise-takes-on-amazons-kindle-and-goodreads-with-its-new-app-for-book-lovers/)). `[INFERRED]` Its attention is moving towards reading itself; review is the oldest part.

### Daily Review: keeping it up, dropping it, does it work

- **The habit rests on a daily cue and very little time.** `[SAID]` About 1 minute or less (blogger, [search snippet](https://www.mickmel.com/a-little-readwise-every-day), 2023, `[OLD]`, page refused); one user's morning email at 9 AM ([2021](https://rishikeshs.com/readwise-review/), `[OLD]`, referral link); ten highlights a day, 1,000 days straight ([blog, 2025-08-07](https://aethermug.com/posts/do-you-remember-what-you-read), no sponsorship). `[CLAIMED]` "Thousands" kept it up over a year without a break ([co-founder, 2021](https://nesslabs.com/readwise-featured-tool), `[OLD]`). That's the only figure anywhere, it's the seller's, and it has no denominator.
- **Streaks cut both ways.** Readwise has streaks and lets users recover a review missed in the last 7 days ([changelog, 2021](https://readwise.io/changelog/stats), `[OLD]`). 3 first-person sources found the streak a weight: one broke it and felt relief, and had been clicking through on busy days to save it ([blog, 2023-03-28](https://www.macsparky.com/blog/2023/03/__trashed-15/), `[OLD]`); one [HN comment](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=38920292) (Jan 2024, `[OLD]`) quit when it turned mindless; one [store review](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/id1476885528) (Dec 2023, `[OLD]`) found reading streaks stressful and hesitated to continue the trial. Against that, the [2025 blogger](https://aethermug.com/posts/do-you-remember-what-you-read) reports 1,000 days with no complaint about them.
- **Those who never start or stop looking.** `[SAID]` One user loved Reader but never built the [review habit](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=36013622) (2023, `[OLD]`); one [always forgets](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44345279) to open such apps (2025); one tried Reader for a month, liked Daily Review, and still found he cared less about highlights than expected and left ([blog, Feb 2026](https://shiveenp-com.pages.dev/blog/my-short-lived-experiment-with-readwise-and-reader)). `[INFERRED]` Silent droppers leave no trace; this is the biggest hole in the evidence.
- **People lower the dose rather than quit.** `[SAID]` A store reviewer says frequency can drop to weekly (snippet, undated); one blogger scans the week's highlights on Mondays ([Oct 2025](https://youvisible.substack.com/p/how-readwise-reader-shapes-a-consistent)). The docs add per-source tuning and an email-off switch.
- **Do they feel it works?** Of 9 HN comments on the review: 7 favourable ([one](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48882343), [two](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=37034103), [three](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41734847), [four](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43195928); two of the seven from 2023 and 2024, `[OLD]`), 1 dropped, 1 never started. In the MPU thread, 7 of about 16 posts keep paying, 2 stopped, and only 1 credits the daily review itself. What they mean by "works" `[SAID]`: resurfacing something forgotten, sparking a new idea, thinking again about a problem. Almost nobody claims retained facts.
  - The most careful user account: it brings back sentence-level detail, but the insights often fade again soon; its value is new connections ([2025 blog](https://aethermug.com/posts/do-you-remember-what-you-read)).
  - A forum dissent: the problem isn't forgetting, it's not using highlights (1 post, [MPU, June 2025](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/in-praise-of-readwise/40974)).
  - Expert view: Andy Matuschak doubts it builds detailed recall. Feedback only sets when a highlight returns, not whether you recalled it. Value is "(slight)" if you're totally passive; paid renewals are the main evidence of worth ([note](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/zAt1K9ARQYguinoHH8cfaqQ), [note](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/z2ewMN8Hzd8gt4qyfQV1ognJ5PQs3CXxDfCJ), undated). The co-founder himself wrote in 2018 that it lacked hard spaced repetition and active recall ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=17717588), `[OLD]`).
  - `[CLAIMED]` The store says it uses "Spaced Repetition and Active Recall"; I found no independent study.
  - It takes time to pay: two to three weeks of use (Asian Efficiency review, July 2026, seen only through [Techpresso](https://academy.techpresso.co/reviews/is-readwise-reader-worth-it)).
  - It needs raw material: not worth it without enough highlights ([2021](https://rishikeshs.com/readwise-review/), `[OLD]`).
- **What they say about the price.** Split. For it, about 7 of 76 HN comments ([one](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47564375), [two](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47306114), [three](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44065482)); about 3 of them value that paying keeps a bootstrapped company alive after Pocket died ([one](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44066066)). Against it, about 5. The objection is mostly about value for partial use: one who only reads ([$120 a year](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44068545)), one who reads too little ([$10 a month](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44695244)). Another priced it at $95.88 a year, tried four readers and stayed ([blog, undated, up to 2026](https://cerebro.pages.dev/posts/finding-a-readwise-reader-replacement)). The founder's reply: discounts exist, and it must charge enough to last ([HN, May 2025](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44065292)). The blogger with 1,000 days pays about $5.59, the cheap tier ([2025](https://aethermug.com/posts/do-you-remember-what-you-read)). `[INFERRED]` The price draws few objections from those who do the review; the objections come from those who use it partly or little.

### Readwise Reader

- **Who it's for `[CLAIMED]`:** "power readers". **Features:** one inbox for articles, PDFs, EPUBs, newsletters, RSS, YouTube and threads; highlighting; text to speech; Ghostreader AI ([page](https://readwise.io/read)).
- **Complaints `[SAID]`:** too much at once: "a firehose" of content and notifications, and left after a month ([blog, 2026](https://shiveenp-com.pages.dev/blog/my-short-lived-experiment-with-readwise-and-reader)); fiddly to set up (1 MPU post, 2025); ugly or unchanged ([1 HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44603876)); store requests: TTS resume, multi-select, a listening queue ([store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1567599761?l=en-us)).- **Price:** in Full, or $7.99 alone, billed yearly, since Oct 2026. Reader alone lacks Daily Review and exports.
- **Why move or leave:** as above. **Surfaces:** it sends notifications, which the same blogger found distracting. No widget found on the store page or in what I read; not confirmed absent. **Watch, lock screen, menu bar:** none found.
- **First sentence:** site: "Readwise makes it easy to revisit and learn from your digital highlights." Store: "Readwise Reader is the first read-it-later app built specifically for power readers."
- **Alive:** yes. Store version 8.28, released the day I read it; 4.6 from 592 ratings.

### Glasp

- **Who it's for `[CLAIMED]`:** people who highlight the web, YouTube, PDFs and Kindle, mostly free, and share them. HN and reviews treat it more as a YouTube summarizer ([2 of 7 HN comments](https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=glasp&tags=comment&hitsPerPage=40), 2023, `[OLD]`).
- **Advantages:** free unlimited highlights; free Kindle sync and Daily Kindle Review email; exports; free MCP connector ([Glasp's own blog, sells it, 2026-08-03](https://glasp.co/posts/best-readwise-alternatives)). It admits Readwise's sync and spaced review are deeper. One Product Hunt review likes the daily email ([5 reviews, 2 to 3 years old](https://www.producthunt.com/products/glasp/reviews)).
- **Complaints `[SAID]`:** few reviews exist. Unrecognized monthly charges, unanswered emails, later refunded (1 store review); Kindle highlights not showing (1, Japanese store, snippet); Safari share button (1 Product Hunt); social highlighting's cold start ([1 HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=28699684), 2021, `[OLD]`).
- **Privacy default:** highlights are public by default, private by choice on every plan ([pricing](https://glasp.co/pricing)). [An affiliate review](https://aisotools.com/blog/glasp-review-2026) says private needs Pro: it disagrees with Glasp's own page.
- **Price:** Free; Pro $12.50 yearly or $15 monthly; Unlimited $30 or $36 ([pricing](https://glasp.co/pricing)). One review reports Pro rose from $12 to $15 on May 1, 2026 (snippet, unconfirmed). No comments found on price.
- **Surfaces:** email; Lock Screen widget with the latest video from followed channels; Home Screen widgets ([store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glasp/id1645763435)). No watch or menu bar found. No reception found.
- **First sentence:** store: "Glasp turns everything you read and watch into knowledge you keep." Site: refused (403).
- **Alive:** yes. Version 3.13.2, Oct 16; 4.5 from 26 ratings: a small iOS presence.

### Matter

- **Who it's for `[CLAIMED]`:** readers on Apple devices who want polished read-later, listening and highlights.
- **Features `[CLAIMED]`:** articles, newsletters, PDFs, YouTube, podcasts as text; voice; highlights, also while listening; shuffle; Quoteshots; sync to notes ([site](https://www.getmatter.com/)). "Rediscover" resurfaces highlights like flash cards ([MacStories, Dec 2023](https://www.macstories.net/club/macstories-weekly-issue-396/app-debuts-395/), `[OLD]`); today's site doesn't mention it. Readwise has the fuller review, says [one comparison](https://www.readless.app/blog/matter-app-pricing-2026) by a seller of a rival.
- **Complaints:** no Android: 1 of 5 Product Hunt reviews and [a review](https://makerstack.co/reviews/matter-review/) with some affiliate funding; audio cutting out (1, [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/matter-7/reviews), 2 to 3 years old); login, slow saves, incomplete articles (2 store reviews, one from 2023, `[OLD]`); price a hurdle (1 store review); uncertain saves ([1 MPU, 2022](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/matter-launches-version-2-0-update-to-the-read-it-later-app/29110), `[OLD]`).
- **Price:** free core. It began charging on Jan 15, 2023, after being free with no plan people could see, which worried MPU users in 2022 (`[OLD]`). Official page: $8 a month, $60 a year. The store lists a confusing set, from $2.99 weekly to a $149.99 Patron tier ([store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/matter-reader-and-highlighter/id1501592184); [Readless, a rival](https://www.readless.app/blog/matter-app-pricing-2026)). Comments on price: 2 reviews call it steep or a hurdle.
- **Why move or leave:** to it from Pocket (store review); away to Reader (1 MPU, 2022) or back to Instapaper (1 MPU, 2022), both `[OLD]`.
- **Surfaces:** a widget exists: release notes fix a blank one and "stale widget highlights" ([store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/matter-reader-and-highlighter/id1501592184)). `[INFERRED]` It shows highlights. No Mac app, watch or lock-screen view found. No reception found.
- **First sentence:** site: "Matter is the modern read-later app for iPhone, iPad, and web." Store: "Finally, a better reading app."
- **Alive:** yes. Version 4.1.18, Oct 23; 4.6 from 1.2K ratings.

## Evidence against

- **Against "the habit lasts":** the long-streak accounts are self-reports by those who stayed, and two reviews have referral links ([2021](https://rishikeshs.com/readwise-review/), [2019](https://www.hackernoon.com/how-to-remember-what-you-read-my-readwise-review-c0173znf), both `[OLD]`). The only number is the co-founder's, from 2021, with no base. The droppers barely write, and Reddit, where they might, wasn't readable.
- **Against "streaks are a weight":** only 3 sources, from 2023 and 2024, and 1,000 days passed with no complaint for one blogger. Readwise still has streaks, with recovery.
- **Against "it works":** favourable accounts describe feelings and sparks, not measured recall. Matuschak and the forum dissent both doubt the claimed effect, and even the 1,000-day blogger says insights fade. A lower bound on value is renewals, which nobody published.
- **Against "the price is contested":** 5 complaints out of 76 comments is a small share, against about 7 who defend it, and HN skews towards those who already pay. Non-users don't show up.- **Against "people come to it for review":** most recent HN flow is about Reader replacing Pocket, not Daily Review.
