# 20 unprompted-cards
Sources read: about 65 (store pages, forums, papers, launch pages). Covered from 2012 to 2026.

Store dates without a year are read as 2026 when the version list implies it `[INFERRED]`. Ratings are per storefront. First sentences are paraphrased (see Blockers).

## Findings

### Do people want this, and keep it on?

- **Wanted, but mostly as counts, not cards.** `[SAID]` Two AnkiMobile threads ask for widgets: about 11 people, [2020–22](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/adding-a-widget-for-the-ios-app/4355) `[OLD]`, and about 13, [2023–July 2026](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-widget/32633). Most requests are due counts, streaks and heatmaps; about 3 of 8 request types ask for a card or random cards. A Reddit post on a widget had 771 upvotes (Jan 2025), and a heatmap post 500+ votes: second-hand, through a search result and that thread. No widget had shipped by [July 2026](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankimobile-widget/32633/27) `[INFERRED]`.
- **Cards in the OS, asked for directly:** [1 wishlist thread](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ios-widgets-notifications-watch-app-other-features/57853) (2025: widgets through the day, a random card as a notification, watch complication), [1 desktop pop-up thread](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/add-on-for-reviewing-card-at-intervals/41130) (Feb 2024, 4 posters) `[OLD]`. `[SAID]` Small numbers.
- **People build it themselves:** `[SAID]` about 4 cases: a [2012 notification script](https://eastasiastudent.net/study/notify-flashcards-01/) `[OLD]`, a [Scriptable widget](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-accountability-widget-for-ios-macos/31983) (2023, no replies) `[OLD]`, a repeating Google Keep reminder (Ask HN, 2020) `[OLD]`, and a [Pushover add-on](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/functional-notifications-are-essential-for-ankimobile/66148) (2025). None reports back on lasting use.
- **The one direct comparison:** [Schneegass et al., 2021](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2109.07944) `[OLD]`, 30 people, 3 weeks. Answers rose about 54% with a task at each unlock and 43% with a persistent notification, against a normal app. Favourite: notification 18 of 30; unlock 7. Least favourite: unlock 15. Answers fell an estimated 14% per day across the study, which the authors read as a possible novelty effect; the unlock app offset part of the fall. Willingness to keep using did not differ significantly. Recall was not measured.
- **Keeping it on after the first weeks: I found no direct evidence.** No source tracks unprompted cards beyond 3 weeks, and none reports a person's use over months.
- **Installs far exceed active use, on one estimate.** WordBit German: 694K installs, about 5.2K monthly actives, under $10K a month, [AppGoblin estimates](https://appgoblin.info/apps/net.wordbit.deen) (read Nov 2026). `[INFERRED]` Under 1% of installs active; a third-party model, and one app of a family.
- **Widgets are a minority habit even among Apple fans.** [TidBITS reader survey](https://tidbits.com/2024/01/15/do-you-use-it-widgets-see-middling-adoption/), about 350 people, Jan 2024 `[OLD]`: 14% use iPhone widgets heavily and "don't use" was the top answer. About half never use widgets on the Mac; 27% use desktop widgets heavily or slightly, 43% Notification Center ones. The writer rarely sees desktop widgets because windows cover them. Self-selected.
- **Repetition dulls notifications.** Duolingo's recurring-reminder work reported about +0.5% daily actives and +2% new-user retention, and warns that repeating a template desensitizes users. Read through [a 2022 newsletter](https://newsletter.vickiboykis.com/archive/duo-the-push-and-the-bandits) and the [paper's search summary](https://paperswithcode.com/paper/a-sleeping-recovering-bandit-algorithm-for) `[OLD]`. Reminders, not cards.
- **Turning off:** I found no opt-out rates. Complaints come through in reviews (below).

### How they do; which died

- **Two big ones, both selling their own content, not the user's cards:** Vocabulary (12.5M+ downloads, [MWM estimate](https://mwm.ai/apps/vocabulary-learn-words-daily/1084540807)) and WordBit Inglés ([10M installs](https://apppricinglab.com/app/google_play/net.wordbit.enes)).
- **The rest are tiny.** `[CLAIMED]` 15 of 16 newer widget, menu-bar, notch and watch apps I checked show under 100 ratings; 8 show none. Only Lingo Widget passes it (102). Many are under 18 months old, so too early to call.
- **New-tab extensions are nearly empty:** Studylib 4K+ users, Flashcard New Tab 114 (last update 2022), Re:Peat 21, FlashTabs 1 (2021), FlashTab 1, Anki New Tab 0.
- **Public revenue:** only the AppGoblin figure. None for Vocabulary, Wunderbar or NotchWise.
- **Died:** Semper, formerly UnlockYourBrain. No update since mid-2017 and gone from iOS, per [one 2019 listing](https://alternativeto.net/software/semper/about) `[OLD]`. No source states a cause. `[INFERRED]` Two things may have counted: [iOS setup needed a copy of each paired app](https://www.iphone-ticker.de/semper-beim-starten-von-apps-vokabeln-lernen-97203/) (2016) `[OLD]`, and iOS has no unlock hook.
- **Stalled or fragile:** Ruzu Pop-ups broke on Anki 2.1.66 and was fixed in Dec 2024 (mirror snippet); its Chrome extension [now shows as unavailable](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ruzu-anki-pop-ups/mpjdjilfcgmndfnailloidpemknemeno/reviews?hl=en) to a signed-out visitor. Flash Cards Widget's last release is 11/04/2025.

### What works, what annoys

Works `[SAID]`:
- No separate session: Wunderbar launch comments, several of 42; one LingoBar review on not being intrusive; one Flashcard Widget review on study without unlocking.
- Pay once, offline: several Wunderbar comments.

Annoys:
- **Being stopped on the way to something else:** the [WordBit review](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/wordbit/) (May 2024, `[OLD]`, affiliate site, score 3.0), 2 participants in Schneegass, and Google's AI summary of WordBit French, on full-screen prompts (search snippet).
- **Too many notifications:** Vocabulary's review summary, an AI digest with sample reviews. One reviewer asks for a once-daily widget refresh.
- **Stale or stuck widgets:** Vocabulary (2025), WidgetCards (2026), AnkiDroid after 2.23.0 (1 report, [Dec 2025](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ankidroid-2-23-0-changelog/67744.md)).
- **Menu bar space:** 3 Wunderbar sources (FAQ, HN, the developer's reply to a store review).
- **Seeing without testing:** Wunderbar's HN thread, and Anki's developer, Aug 2025.
- **Backlog numbers:** in [a 2024 Anki thread](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/notification-options/50544), one user on due counts among other alerts: "it adds to my stress instead of as a daily reminder."
- **Unwanted in meetings:** profanity in the menu bar during screen sharing (1 HN comment). NotchWise sells hiding as a feature.

### On the Mac

Native: Wunderbar, NotchWise, LingoBar, VocaBar, Word Drop, GlimpseWords, Purrfect Memory, My Vocabulary (Mac and Windows), Obsidian Popup Review (needs Obsidian running), ankistatsonmenu (counts only). Mac-capable, unclear on Mac widgets: Swiftflip, Flashcard Maker. iPad builds marked not verified for macOS: Vocabulary, Lingo Widget, MemoryPush, Top Note, WidgetCards, Flash Cards Widget. Not on Mac: WordBit (except through an emulator, as [BlueStacks advertises](https://www.bluestacks.com/apps/educational/wordbit-ingles-on-pc.html)), FloFla, AnkiDroid. Mac apps found with a user-deck FSRS scheduler and self-appearing cards: only NotchWise; Wunderbar has SRS and custom lists.

## Sheets

**NotchWise** ([store](https://apps.apple.com/app/notchwise/id6760242442), [site](https://absastreon.github.io/projects/notchwise.html), [launch](https://www.producthunt.com/products/notchwise))
- For: Anki users on a Mac. First sentence: store, turn the notch into a review surface; site, flashcards living in the notch that fit around work.
- Features `[CLAIMED]`: cards in the notch or a floating widget, surfaced in workflow gaps; hover to flip, 1–4 to rate; .apkg, CSV, AnkiConnect; FSRS-4.5 and SM-2; context decks; hides during screen share, Focus, presentations; offline, no account.
- Complaints: none on record: no ratings, 3 launch points, 2 followers.
- Price: free for 1 deck and 50 cards; Pro $19.99 (store), £14.99 (launch page). No history found.
- Move/leave: [maker's blog](https://absastreon.github.io/writing/how-i-built-notchwise.html), own product: a short Anki session took him about 20 minutes; a menu-bar version felt too hidden, a floating window too pushy. One person.

- For: language learners working on a Mac. First sentence: site, memorize common words on the menu bar with SRS; store, a new word each minute.
- Features: a word in the menu bar; about 10 languages; 4 rating buttons and custom list import (v2.0–2.1); spoken examples; offline.

- Price: launched $3.99, now $4.99 / €5.99; one-time. Paying for a small app drew mixed comments.
- Surfaces: menu bar only.
- Reception: 60 points, 42 comments on HN, Apr 2024 `[OLD]`; 4.5 from 57 per its site `[CLAIMED]`; store shows no overview.

## Words people use

Terms in close paraphrase, except the one quote above: bothered at wanting the phone quickly (Schneegass); intrusive, full-screen (WordBit summary); buzzing several times an hour, no once-daily option (Vocabulary); robotic voices, word disappears, false confidence (Wunderbar); too hidden, too pushy, closed Anki after three cards (NotchWise maker, own product); study without unlocking (Flashcard Widget); not intrusive (LingoBar).

## Evidence against

- **Against demand:** Anki's developer doubted widgets in [Aug 2025](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/ios-widgets-notifications-watch-app-other-features/57853): passive exposure without recall does little, and spreading reviews adds context switching. In [Ask HN, 2020](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22484060) `[OLD]`, about 8 said they don't need memorization and about 8 blamed card-making; about 2 mentioned reminders, none widgets. [Pleco's staff](https://plecoforums.com/threads/reminder-to-review-flashcards.6318/latest) (2019) `[OLD]` called overdue reminders oversold. [A commenter](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/functional-notifications-are-essential-for-ankimobile/66148) said power users don't need them.
- **Some want force, not nudges:** [AnkiLock](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/ankilock/id6748466549) and [FareGate](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/block-social-media-until-daily-reviews-are-done/68810) lock apps until reviews are done; no rating data.
- **Against "it annoys":** the notification app was the favourite. MIT's [WaitChatter and WaitSuite](https://news.mit.edu/2017/learn-language-while-you-wait-for-wifi-0417) `[OLD]` taught about 4 words a day over 2 weeks at waiting moments, and its author says users strayed less to social media. Her caveat: not a path to fluency.
- **Against "the small apps failed":** most are young and ratings per storefront undercount. Wunderbar keeps shipping over two years on. Vocabulary has lasted ten years and WordBit is still updated, both with their own vocabulary content, not users' cards.
- **Against the widget survey:** readers of one Apple site, 2024.

## Blockers

- **Reddit:** only via search results. By mistake I opened one third-party Reddit snapshot, `reddit.sentinel-team.org`, early on; it was off-topic and nothing here uses it. I opened no other mirror.
- **Google Play:** pages came back truncated, so no WordBit reviews read directly. Lucas could open Play and filter WordBit's reviews for "lock" or "notification".
- **Refused:** marlvel.ai, craft.co, root-nation.com, Softpedia (403); De Gruyter (405); languagelearning.stackexchange.com; the AppWisp and AppFollow pages (connection failed).
- **PDFs:** WaitSuite, WaitChatter and Duolingo's KDD paper came back as binary, so I used press and summaries. Lucas could paste their results sections.
