# 21 word-a-day
Sources read: 38 (9 store pages; 6 apps' Apple review feeds, 650 reviews; 6 AppGoblin profiles and a trend page; 17 sites, articles, trackers and listings). Covered from 2018 to 2026.

Method note: Apple's public feeds show US reviews only, so English speakers. I had a reading tool count them, so counts are approximate and quotes passed through it. Written reviews skew unhappy compared with star averages. All store pages and feeds read 2026-11-05.

Feeds: [Vocabulary](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=1084540807/sortby=mostrecent/json) (pages 1-5 and [most helpful](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=1084540807/sortby=mosthelpful/json)), [Motivation](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=876080126/sortby=mostrecent/json) (recent and [most helpful](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=876080126/sortby=mosthelpful/json)), [I am](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=874656917/sortby=mostrecent/json), [Word of the Day](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=987136347/sortby=mostrecent/json) (pages 1-2), [Merriam-Webster](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=399452287/sortby=mostrecent/json), [Dictionary.com](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=308750436/sortby=mostrecent/json).

## Findings

### Sheet: Vocabulary (Monkey Taps)
- **Who for:** people after rarer English words; a 2025 review calls the audience narrow, advanced enthusiasts, not general learners. [INFERRED by that writer] ([Woshipm, 2025-09-23](https://www.woshipm.com/evaluating/6272227.html); the site sells courses.)
- **Features:** 10,000+ words by level and topic, a TikTok-like swipe feed, lock-screen widget, games and quizzes, wallpapers, saved words. [CLAIMED] ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vocabulary-learn-words-daily/id1084540807))
- **Complaints (250 latest reviews, 2026-07-26 to 11-02):** 110 of 250 are 1-2 stars, against a 4.8 store average from 222K ratings. About 82 of 250 complain of price, trials or charges. Many describe being billed after a trial or cancellation, or the reminder not arriving. About 14 mention too many or badly timed notifications. Paywalled saved words and practice recur. [SAID]
- **Price:** free with ads; Premium listed at $14.99/month, $35.99-$59.99/year, $149.99 lifetime, plus other variants. [CLAIMED] ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vocabulary-learn-words-daily/id1084540807); [RecurDash, checked 2026-09-28](https://recurdash.com/guides/how-to-cancel-vocabulary-learn-words-daily), which promotes its own subscription tracker.) 3-day trial into an annual plan (RecurDash).
- **How the price changed:** a 2022 reviewer cites $19/year ([JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1084540807/vocabulary-learn-new-words/contact)) `[OLD]`; a 2024 reviewer $5/month and $30/year, with a $15/year offer (App Store page); 2024 renewal capture $34.98 (RecurDash). Today's list reaches $59.99. Buyers report different prices for one product: $14.99 advertised, $21.99 charged (1 review, 2026-10-25). [SAID] Rising list price plus heavy price testing. [INFERRED]
- **Why people come:** a daily habit with little effort [SAID]; store visibility through Apple's own feature ([Apple story, undated](https://apps.apple.com/py/ipad/story/id1675956696)) [INFERRED]. **Why they leave:** billing, ads on the lock screen (1), and paid access to their own saved words. [SAID] One reviewer says the widget stopped updating and most list features went paid ([2025-09-14](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vocabulary-learn-words-daily/id1084540807)).
- **Surfaces:** notifications (user sets a count), Home and Lock Screen widgets, Apple Watch complication; no menu-bar item seen. The widget is a headline feature, with about 27 of 250 reviews on it, mixed: hard setup, too fast a rotation, no once-a-day option.
- **First sentence:** company site ([Monkey Taps](https://monkeytaps.net)): "Self-care, self-love, self-growth." Store: "The word of the day on your Lock Screen: learn a new word every unlock." Subtitle: "Word of the day widget & games".
- **Alive:** yes; 7.7.0 about 2026-11-04, released since 2016-02-19. Weekly releases ([Bitrise](https://bitrise.io/resources/tools/app-navigator/apps/ios/com.MonkeyTaps.Vocabulary)).

- **Complaints (50 latest, 2026-10-08 to 11-03):** 32 are 5-star, 12 are 1-star. About 17 of 50 concern price, nearly all an unexpected yearly charge. 1 says the widget cycles the same quotes; a few call quotes generic. [SAID]

- **Price:** $3.99 to $47.99 across SKUs, no plan labels. [CLAIMED] Soft paywall with 3-day trial and a billing timeline, after a 41-step onboarding ([ScreensDesign, undated](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/motivation-daily-quotes)). 22,000 KRW/year in 2024 ([Monday Morning newsletter, 2024-02-19](https://maily.so/mondaymorning/posts/mjz67nxnowk)).

- **First sentence:** store: "Motivation on your Lock Screen: a reminder of who you're becoming."
- **Alive:** 7.8.0, about 2026-11-05; released 2014-05-13. 4.8 from 1.1M US ratings.

### Do they teach anything, by users' own account?
- Many say yes. About 63 of 250 recent Vocabulary reviews, and about 35 of 50 most helpful, praise learning or an expanded vocabulary. About 30 of 100 on Word of the Day do too. [SAID] None gives a test or measure. [INFERRED]
- About 20 of 250 recent Vocabulary reviews, and about 12 of 50 most helpful, say they forget, cannot review, or find review paywalled. [SAID] They split into: bring old words back; slow the feed to one word a day or a week; let me mark known words (about 9 of 250 on repeats and known words); stop charging for practice.
- Motivation and I am users claim feeling, not learning: about 45 of 100 recent reviews. [SAID]
- Independent evidence is thin. A [3-week field study (Mensch und Computer 2021)](https://dl.gi.de/items/0a215a4f-6428-4d47-9b6c-39d39685a2df/full) found unlock and notification vocabulary apps raised answers over a standard app, with an exposure-versus-disturbance trade-off; its abstract reports no retention results. I found no study on retention from lock-screen exposure. A 2023 writer [credits Vocabulary](https://fueled.com/blog/vocabulary-helps-expand-well-know/) with more staying power than word calendars (Fueled is an app agency; 1 person).

### Do those users know spaced repetition, or want it?
- They do not use the term. "Spaced repetition" appears 0 times in 300 Vocabulary reviews and 0 times in 100 Word of the Day reviews, an app that advertises it. "Anki" and "SRS" appear 0 times, but I checked those on only 50 Vocabulary reviews. [SAID, by absence]
- Some describe the need in plain words, as above: about 8% of recent Vocabulary reviews, 24% of the most helpful. [INFERRED]
- A [2024 SlashGear roundup, updated 2026-05-21](https://www.slashgear.com/1544971/apps-to-enhance-vocabulary-2024/) never uses the term either, and no forum thread linking word-a-day apps to spaced repetition turned up in 3 searches.

## Evidence against
- **Billing anger is not the whole picture.** Store averages stay at 4.7-4.8, and written complaints are a tiny share of ratings. Motivation and I am are 62-64% 5-star even in recent written reviews. Long-term payers call them worth it. No independent complaint trail turned up on Trustpilot or elsewhere, and billing runs through Apple, so some "charged after cancelling" reviews may be timing misunderstandings: RecurDash says to cancel 24 hours ahead. [INFERRED]
- **"Users want review" is a minority signal.** About 92% of recent Vocabulary reviews do not ask for it. Vocabulary already offers practice, and its most helpful reviews include people who say words stick.
- **Spaced repetition does not protect an app.** Word of the Day claims it and has the worst sentiment of the learning apps here, with low estimated revenue, and users ask for simplicity. WOTD markets the absence of flashcards. Causes are not separable from its ads. [INFERRED]
- **The revenue picture is weak.** The ad share (54-100%) is modelled and sits uneasily with paid complaints; Dictionary.com shows 100% ads while a Remove Ads purchase exists. Vocabulary's estimates run from $50K to $800K. The newsletter's $6M names no source.
- **Vocabulary's slowdown is soft.** AppGoblin labels installs declining but shows 206K a month, and Adapty ranks it #35 Top Free in US Education (2026-10-23).
