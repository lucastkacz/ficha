# Content guide

How Claude drafts Lucas's cards in a refine session (`/refine`). Lucas approves every card before it's saved; this guide is what makes a draft read like one he'd write. The terms are `CONTEXT.md`'s.

## The four rules

Every card follows them, whatever its type.

1. **Rioplatense Spanish, with "vos".** "Mirá lo que viene", "lo vas haciendo vos". Neutral words where Rioplatense has none of its own.
2. **One idea per card.** A capture with two ideas makes two cards, and a back that needs "y además" is two cards.
3. **A one-line front, and a back of at most 40 words.** The review panel shows both without scrolling. Count the back's words before proposing it.
4. **Words from other languages, explained in Spanish.** The front keeps the word as it's written in its language; the back says which language it is and what it means.

## Each type

Pick the type by what Lucas has to remember, not by the capture's form: "cuándo llegamos a la Luna" is a question, and "ubuntu" can be a concept.

- **Word** (`word`): the front is the word alone, as it's written, in lowercase unless its language capitalizes it ("Schadenfreude"). The back is its meaning in plain words. For a word from another language, the back starts with the language ("Palabra alemana: …"). Its origin goes in only when it helps remember the word.
- **Concept** (`concept`): the front is the concept's name, with its article when Spanish uses one ("La navaja de Ockham"). The back says what it is in a sentence or two, and then, if there's room, who came up with it or an example.
- **Quote** (`quote`): the front is the quote, word for word, with its final punctuation. The back says who said it and where, and what it means. In a review, Lucas rates whether he had understood it, so the meaning is the core of the back.
- **Question** (`question`): the front is a question with one short answer. The back starts with the answer, and then says why or how in a sentence.

## Tags

A tag is a subject, in Spanish and in lowercase: "lenguaje", "filosofía", "historia". One per card usually, and up to three. Reuse the tags Lucas's cards already have (`ficha cards`) before making up a new one, and propose a new tag in so many words, so Lucas sees it. A card that fits no subject goes without tags.

## Sources

A card takes its capture's source as Lucas wrote it, and the review panel shows it small under the back. The back names the work only when the card needs it, as a quote does ("Antonio Machado, en «Campos de Castilla»").

## Flagged cards and expansions

- **A flagged card**: change what Lucas's note asks for and keep the rest as it was, so he recognizes the card. When the note says the card is no longer worth it, propose burying it.
- **An expansion**: when what it adds fits on its card, within the 40 words, propose the card's new content (`ficha approve-expansion`). When it's another idea, like a second meaning, propose a card of its own (`ficha approve`).

## Examples

### Word

> **serendipia**
> Un hallazgo valioso que se da por casualidad, cuando estabas buscando otra cosa.
> _lenguaje_

> **Schadenfreude**
> Palabra alemana: la alegría por la desgracia ajena. Viene de «Schaden», daño, y «Freude», alegría.
> _lenguaje_

### Concept

> **ubuntu**
> Una idea de los pueblos bantúes del sur de África: una persona es persona a través de las demás. Se suele resumir como «soy porque somos».
> _filosofía_

> **La falacia del costo hundido**
> Seguir con algo solo por lo que ya invertiste, aunque no te convenga. Lo gastado no vuelve: para decidir, mirá lo que viene.
> _psicología_ · Pensar rápido, pensar despacio

### Quote

> **Caminante, no hay camino, se hace camino al andar.**
> Antonio Machado, en «Campos de Castilla»: el camino no está trazado de antemano; lo vas haciendo vos, con cada paso.
> _literatura_ · Campos de Castilla

> **Solo sé que no sé nada.**
> Se le atribuye a Sócrates, a partir de la «Apología» de Platón: reconocer lo que no sabés es el primer paso para aprender.
> _filosofía_

### Question

> **¿Por qué el cielo es azul?**
> Porque el aire dispersa mucho más la luz azul, de longitud de onda corta, que la roja, y esa luz dispersada te llega desde todo el cielo. Se llama dispersión de Rayleigh.
> _ciencia_ · Una charla de divulgación

> **¿En qué año llegó el ser humano a la Luna?**
> En 1969, con el Apolo 11. El 20 de julio, Neil Armstrong fue el primero en pisarla, y Buzz Aldrin lo siguió poco después.
> _historia_
