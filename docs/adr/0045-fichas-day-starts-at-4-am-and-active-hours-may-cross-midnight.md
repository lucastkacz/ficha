# Ficha's day starts at 4 AM, and active hours may cross midnight

On 2026-11-27 Lucas asked for Ficha for night owls (#562): active hours that cross midnight, such as 20 to 3, and a day of Ficha's, with its new cards, that doesn't break at midnight in the middle of that night. He named the case of a new card shown at 23:59 and answered at 0:00, which counted toward the next day. Until then active hours were one span inside the calendar's day, ending at midnight at the latest, and the new cards a day, the finish date and the widget's next new card counted by the calendar's days, which a test fixed as decided. In the debate "Horario nocturno", two rounds the same evening, he chose a day that starts at 4 AM, as Anki's does, and wrote in his notes that a hidden setting in Advanced should change its hour; that Settings keep its two menus, with a bar below them that shows what the user set; and that when the hours pass the day's start, Ficha should offer to change it right there, rather than send the user to another tab. He let the recommendations stand for the hour syncing and for the bar showing Ficha's day. He approved the decision the same day, in the artifact "Decisión del horario nocturno", with no notes on its pieces. It changes ADR 0034 in part, whose new cards a day and finish date counted by the calendar's days; and ADR 0017 in part, as ADR 0025 left it, whose settings that decide progress gain one. It follows ADR 0003, whose appearances come within active hours, and ADR 0025, whose cards fall due at a time, not by the day: Ficha's day decides only the new cards a day and the words for days.

- **Ficha's day starts at 4:00.** It runs from 4:00 to 4:00 the next day, by the device's clock and time zone, as Anki's "Next day starts at" does by default. A new card counts toward the day its first review is answered in, as before and as in Anki (provisional). The pace's new cards a day, the finish date, How fast?'s "today", the widget's next new card and the Cards window's days count by it. One rule for all (provisional): for a user whose hours are 9 to 22 nothing changes, since the debate's simulation brought the same new cards at the same times with the day at 4:00 as at midnight.
- **Its hour syncs, and hides in Advanced.** The hour is one of the settings that decide progress, so a new card counts toward the same day on each device: it syncs as a field of their iCloud record, taking the latest change, as the pace does. Settings' Advanced tab changes it, as "Next day starts at", Anki's words, among Anki's settings (provisional). The iPhone keeps the default settings, 4:00 among them, until its stage, as it keeps the default pace (provisional).
- **Active hours may cross midnight.** Settings' Start at and End at take any hour but the same one, and End at says "next day" when it comes before Start at (provisional). From 20 to 3, appearances may come from 20:00 to 2:59, without a break at midnight. Active hours stay each device's own (ADR 0017), and the hours stored before read as they were (provisional).
- **A bar shows Ficha's day.** Under the two menus, in Settings' Interruptions tab, a bar from the day's start to the same hour the next day shows the active hours inside it. When the active hours hold the day's start, as 22 to 6 does with the day at 4:00, the bar shows them split at its ends, says "Your hours pass the start of Ficha's day." and offers a button, "Start the day at 6:00", with the hour the active hours end at, which sets the day's start there (provisional: the texts, and where the button puts the day).

## Considered options

- The day at midnight, as before: a night from 20 to 3 is cut in two, the new cards met after midnight count toward the next day, and the first night brings twice the new cards (in the debate's simulation, 4 the first night at Steady, then 2 a night, always after midnight).
- The day starting when active hours start: after waking the user is still on the day before, and each device's own hours would give it a day of its own.
- A "Day starts at" setting beside active hours: a second hour for every user to think about, when 4:00 fits nearly all; Lucas wanted it hidden in Advanced.
- Each device its own day: the same new card would count toward different days on the Mac and the iPhone.
- A 24-hour bar dragged at both ends, in place of the menus: the menus are what Settings already has, and the bar below them shows the result.
- A clock's day, 0 to 24, on the bar: it would show midnight, which no longer cuts anything.
- When the hours pass the day's start: the bar warning and the user changing it in Advanced, Ficha moving it on its own on that device, or End at stopping at 4:00. Lucas asked for the change offered right there.
- Cards due by the day, at 4 AM as in Anki: still rejected, as ADR 0025 has it.

## Consequences

- #562 becomes the Feature for Ficha's day, in Core › Scheduling: FichaCore gets one way to tell a time's Ficha day, which the new cards a day, the queue's finish dates, the widget's next new card, the next day of the iPhone's planned appearances and the words for days use in place of the calendar's start of day; and the hour becomes a field of the settings that decide progress, stored with its change time and synced. A Feature in the same node lets active hours cross midnight. A Feature in Mac › Settings, after both, brings the menus without their limit, the bar with its warning and its button, and Advanced's "Next day starts at".
- The test that fixed "the day's new cards start over at midnight" changes with #562.
- iCloud's Production schema gets the day's start field before the next `make upload` ("A new record type or field in iCloud" in `docs/development.md`).
- The web mock changes with each node, as Lucas's rule of 2026-11-25 asks: its engine's day starts at 4:00 and its active hours may cross midnight, and the desktop mock's Interruptions and Advanced show the bar and the setting.
- `GLOSSARY.md` gains Ficha's day, and its Active hours and Settings that decide progress change. `PLIEGO.md` names it in What it is and in Core › Scheduling's line.
- Later: active hours on the iPhone, in its stage. A card due after the active hours still waits for the next night, as before.
- Standard practice: Anki's "Next day starts at", 4 AM by default (Anki's manual, Preferences, Scheduler). Our own: the bar of Ficha's day under the menus, with its warning and button.
