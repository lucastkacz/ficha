# Cards appear when they're due, not on a timer

The review panel shows up on its own when cards are due, bringing along those about to be, within active hours, spaced apart, and never during a suppression such as a meeting. The obvious alternative, a timer such as "every two hours", either interrupts with nothing to review or makes due cards wait for the next tick. Instead of a time budget, an appearance holds only a few cards, so a backlog drains over several short appearances instead of one long one.

## Consequences

- New cards come in one per appearance, and only when due cards leave room: a backlog also pauses new cards.
- Priority tags (#206) make one exception: a new card with a priority tag only waits for due cards with a priority tag, and comes before the other due cards.
- With nothing due, a single new card is enough for an appearance.
- How many cards an appearance holds, how far ahead it looks and how far apart appearances are, are settings; the MVP spec (#4) holds their defaults.
