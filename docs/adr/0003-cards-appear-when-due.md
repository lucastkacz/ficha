# Cards appear when they're due, not on a timer

The review panel shows up on its own when cards are due, and brings along those due within the next 20 minutes. It only does so within active hours, at least 30 minutes after the last appearance closed, and never during a suppression: a meeting, a video or a full-screen app. The obvious alternative, a timer such as "every two hours", either interrupts with nothing to review or makes due cards wait for the next tick. An appearance has no time budget, since the panel waits for Lucas, but it holds at most five cards, so a backlog drains over several short appearances instead of one long one.

## Consequences

- New cards come in one per appearance, up to five a day, and only when due cards leave room under the cap: a backlog also pauses new cards.
- With nothing due, a single new card is enough for an appearance.
- Every number here is a default that Lucas can change in Settings, as is which suppressions apply.
