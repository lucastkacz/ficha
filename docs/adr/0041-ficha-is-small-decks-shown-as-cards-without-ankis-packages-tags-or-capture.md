# Ficha is small decks, shown as cards, without Anki's packages, tags or Capture

ADR 0032 kept Anki inside Ficha, its decks and tags, FSRS, the four ratings a review stores and its packages, and gave Ficha a face of its own: the review panel, the pace and a few decks of its own. It left the Cards window, the menu and the inbox as they were, and the Cards window was still Anki's Browser almost whole, about half the Mac app's Swift. On 2026-11-26 Lucas asked to simplify the app further, to make it more his own and less a clone of Anki, and said what it is: an app to learn simple things, bite-sized and pretty, such as pretty words, paintings or the world's flags, and not much more. In the debate "Ficha más simple", two rounds the same night, he chose how far that goes; in his notes he wrote that he never used Capture of his own will, that it solved a problem he didn't have and could go with its code, and that a card in the Cards window should show its front's picture. He approved the decision the same day, in the artifact "Ficha más simple: decisión". It changes ADR 0032 in part, whose Anki inside kept its tags and its packages; ADR 0012 in part, whose cards came from captures and from files; ADR 0024 in part, whose cards carry tags and card types on their face and whose decks can be priority decks; ADR 0033 in part, whose card detail shows FSRS's numbers; and ADR 0035 in part, whose Welcome page offers an Anki file. It makes ADRs 0013 and 0015, Anki's packages coming in, and ADRs 0021 and 0022, tags inside tags and the sidebar's tags, obsolete.

- **Ficha's decks and the user's own.** A user learns from Ficha's decks (ADR 0040) and from decks of their own, made in the Cards window. The Welcome page offers Ficha's decks or a deck of one's own, then How fast? (ADR 0034).
- **Anki's packages neither come in nor go out**, and neither do text files or zips (provisional). The Import and Export sheets go, with Anki's readers in FichaCore and FichaData, and with zstd. Inside, Ficha keeps its decks, FSRS from Anki's own library and the four ratings each review stores (ADR 0025, ADR 0026, ADR 0039). Ficha's own decks still come in through the engine's import of their files, which nothing else uses (ADR 0035, ADR 0040).
- **The Cards window shows a deck's cards as cards.** A grid, each card showing its front's picture when it has one, or else its front's text. The sidebar lists decks alone, each with its row from #491. The table and its thirteen columns go (provisional). New Card stays, with a front, a back, a picture or a sound, and its deck (provisional).
- **A card says in words when it's back and how often it was seen.** Its interval, stability, difficulty and retrievability wait under Details, out of sight, as Anki's settings wait in Settings' Advanced tab.
- **Only decks on the face.** Tags and card types leave the sidebar, the card form, the card's detail and the search. The database keeps them, unseen (provisional). Before the build that hides them, Lucas's tags become decks in a refine session he starts (provisional).
- **Capture and the inbox go.** Capture's window, its shortcut, its line in the menu and its row in Settings, the review panel's follow-up, and the inbox with its expansions and duplicates leave the app, as does capturing on the iPhone. Flag stays: a flagged card leaves the reviews and shows in its deck, with its note, until it's fixed. The `ficha` CLI adds a card straight into a deck, `ficha add`, so Claude keeps making Lucas's cards in a refine session (provisional); its capture, inbox, approve, approve-expansion and discard commands go. Before the build that removes the inbox, Lucas's is emptied in a refine session (provisional).
- **A shorter menu.** Review now, the deck in progress with its count, such as "Types of clouds · 9 of 14", Cards…, Settings… and Quit, and the warnings of a failed backup and a full iCloud when they hold. Prioritize and the priority deck leave the app: the queue's Put it first puts a deck's new cards first (ADR 0034). Diagnostics moves to Settings' Advanced tab.
- **Set aside, for a deck or for a card.** Bury goes into it: a card set aside is today's burial under that name, with no migration (provisional), and putting it back leaves it where it stood.
- **Each piece's node deletes what the piece drops**: its code, its tests, its strings and its words in the glossary, not only its buttons, and the web mock changes in the same PR. The database deletes nothing: tags, card types, captures and the priority field stay in its tables (provisional), since iCloud's Production schema never deletes a field (`CLAUDE.md`, Known gotchas); a later decision may take them out.

## Considered options

- Anki inside as ADR 0032 has it, its packages coming in and going out with their tags: their import and export, and the tags, are about a sixth of the app's Swift without its tests, counted by file name, for people who already have Anki.
- Only Ficha's decks, with nothing for the user to make: Lucas makes his own cards, with Claude.
- A deck's cards as a list, a row each: a deck of 14 to 50 cards fits whole as cards, as it does in the panel.
- Decks and loose tags, found with a `#` in the search: without Anki's packages, a tag serves no one, and a refine session already turns tags into decks.
- The inbox kept for flagged cards alone: a box of its own for one kind of thing.
- Set aside and Bury kept apart, or neither: both take cards out of the reviews without losing them, and deleting a card loses what was learned.
- A card's numbers in sight, as ADR 0033 has them, or gone: they stay for whoever looks, out of sight.

## Consequences

- #454 and #457, Anki's packages coming in and going out, #470, a package opening the Import sheet, and #106, Capture on the iPhone, close as not planned, each with a comment that names this record. #467's Welcome page loses the Anki file, and #492's Settings lose Capture's shortcut row and take Diagnostics in Advanced.
- The work goes in issues by node: `ficha add` first (Mac › CLI) and the refine skill without captures (Environment › Method), then the Cards window's pieces (Mac › Cards), the menu (Mac › Menu) and the import's removal (Mac › Cards › Import).
- `PLIEGO.md` says it: in What it is; in its areas, where iPhone › Capture goes, Mac › Cards › Import holds Ficha's deck files alone and Mac › Menu loses Capture and Diagnostics; in its plano, where FichaData loses zstd, FichaCore its reading of Anki, the app the instructions for an AI the Import sheet copied, and rule 4 names set-asides for burials. `GLOSSARY.md`'s words change with the nodes that build each piece: Capture, the inbox, expansions, follow-ups, duplicates, Bury, the priority deck, tags inside tags and card types go, and Flag and Set aside change.
- ADR 0011 told "not for now" from "not anymore": one verb, Set aside, now takes a deck or a card out of the reviews until it's put back.
- The iPhone takes the same direction when it starts (ADR 0019, #97): no capture, and its Cards tab shows cards.
- Later, each in a debate of its own: the app's new look, tried as many options in the web mock, and the codebase's structure once these nodes are built.
- Standard practice: a card's picture as its tile, as photo and flashcard apps show them, and settings out of sight behind a disclosure. Our own: no import or export at all, decks without tags, no capture or inbox, and Set aside for a deck or a card.
