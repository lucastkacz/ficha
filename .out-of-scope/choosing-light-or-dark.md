# Choosing light or dark

Ficha follows the system's light or dark appearance. It has no setting or button of its own for it.

## Why this is out of scope

Lucas turned the idea down on 2026-11-03, following Apple's advice. The Human Interface Guidelines' "Dark Mode" page says "Avoid offering an app-specific appearance setting": people would have to adjust more than one setting to get the appearance they want, and may think the app is broken when it doesn't respond to their systemwide choice.
