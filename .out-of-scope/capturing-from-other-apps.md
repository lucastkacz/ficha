# Capturing from other apps

Text comes into Ficha through its capture window, which the capture shortcut opens from any app (#17): Lucas types or pastes what he wants to remember. Ficha adds no "Capture in Ficha" item to the Services menu of macOS and no Ficha to the Share menu of Safari, Books or other apps, and it doesn't read the text in a photo or a screenshot.

## Why this is out of scope

Lucas read both ideas in the triage of 2026-10-22 and isn't interested in them.
