# Capturing from other apps

On the Mac, Lucas captures by typing or pasting: in the capture window, which the capture shortcut opens from any app (#17), or with `ficha capture` from a terminal. Ficha adds no "Capture in Ficha" item to the Services menu of macOS, and no Ficha to the Share menu of Safari, Books or other apps.

This note covers the Mac. The iPhone's share sheet isn't decided here: #97 leaves it out of the iPhone app's first stage.
