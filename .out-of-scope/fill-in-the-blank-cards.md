# Fill-in-the-blank cards

Ficha has no card type for fill-in-the-blank cards, like Anki's cloze cards, where a sentence written once gives a card for each gap. A card of any type already does the job: its front shows the sentence with "[...]" where the gap goes, and its back the whole sentence.

## Why this is out of scope

Lucas decided in #86's grilling, on 2026-10-23, that the cards Ficha has cover it. Anki shows a cloze card as a front with "[...]" and a back with the whole sentence, which is what an import copies (ADR 0013), and each gap is already a card of its own in Anki. Only writing the sentence once for several gaps is lost.
