# Ficha Privacy Policy

Ficha is a flashcard app for the Mac and the iPhone, made by Lucas Ariel Tkacz. This policy covers both.

## Ficha collects nothing

Ficha doesn't collect, share or sell any data about you or your cards. It has no analytics, no tracking and no ads, and no accounts: you never sign up or sign in.

## Your cards stay on your devices

Your cards, with their images and sounds, your reviews, the inbox and your settings, stay on your devices.

When you turn on sync, your cards, or only those in the decks you choose, also go to your private iCloud, in your own Apple Account, so each of your devices has the same ones. A card you stop syncing leaves iCloud, all but the name of its deck, which tells your other devices why it left, and stays on each device that has it. Apple's iCloud keeps them under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer can't see them. Ficha's backups stay on the device that saved them.

## The network

Ficha makes no network connections of its own. To draw SVG images, such as those in some Anki decks, it uses WebKit, Apple's web engine, which may use the network. Ficha has it draw only images already on your Mac, and loads nothing an image points to on the internet.

## Crash reports

If you share analytics with app developers in your Mac's or iPhone's settings, Apple may send the developer reports of Ficha's crashes, which Apple gathers and which don't include your cards. You can stop sharing them in those settings, under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

## Deleting your data

Delete cards in Ficha whenever you like: with sync on, a card that syncs goes from iCloud and your other devices too, and one that doesn't goes from that device alone, while iCloud keeps the name of its deck if it synced before. To delete everything Ficha keeps in your iCloud, open iCloud's settings on any of your devices, manage its storage, and delete Ficha's data there.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, this page will say so, with the date of the change. To ask about it, open an issue at [github.com/lucastkacz/ficha](https://github.com/lucastkacz/ficha/issues).
